# Hub — 50/50: Turn-Away Containment (Stop the Run, Stabilize the Trade)

## 1. Purpose
50/50 is a containment hub whose primary value is **turn-away control**:
- when the opponent’s best defense is to rotate away and flee,
- 50/50 converts the exchange into a **stable trade state** where exits slow down,
- enabling either systematic finishing work or controlled transition to a more dominant hub.

Operational intent: **contain, deny run, then select.**

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can establish a 50/50-style entanglement where:
  - the opponent cannot freely turn away and disengage,
  - and you can deny one-step knee-line clearance.
- Common arrival pathways:
  - failed inside-senkaku stabilization,
  - opponent turn-away attempt mid-entry,
  - rotational scrambles where containment is safer than chasing.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
You are “in hub” when:
- turn-away/run is denied (rotation does not create distance escape),
- knee line cannot clear in one action,
- you can hold 50/50 stability **3 seconds**,
- you can route to:
  - finishing sequences, or
  - multi-leg containment (double 50) if opponent keeps slipping,
  - or criss-cross if backside stability is required.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**ARRIVE → SEAT 50/50 → DENY TURN-AWAY → DENY KNEE-LINE CLEAR → CONSOLIDATE → ROUTE / FINISH**

50/50 is not “finish-now”; it is **stop-the-run** and stabilize the decision.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Turn-Away Gate (primary)
If the opponent can rotate away and create space, 50/50 is not seated.
- **Default action:** keep leg connection tight; follow rotation; re-seat before attacking.

### 5.2 Knee Line Gate
If knee line can clear in one action, you are not consolidated.
- **Default action:** re-seat lower and add redundancy before progressing.

### 5.3 Pull-Away Gate (disengage)
If the opponent can disengage to two steps, containment failed.
- **Default action:** clamp and keep contact; if lost, route to re-capture systems rather than chase.

### 5.4 “Trade-State” Gate (don’t overreach)
Overreaching to finish can re-open the run lane.
- **Default action:** stabilize 3 seconds, then progress.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Opponent is turning away repeatedly**  
   → **Default:** choose 50/50 to stop the run.

2) **You can’t keep inside-senkaku seated**  
   → **Default:** transition to 50/50 containment rather than lose contact.

3) **Opponent is slippery and clears knee line quickly**  
   → **Default:** route up to double 50 (multi-leg control).

4) **Opponent forces backside pushing contests**  
   → **Default:** route to criss-cross stability.

5) **Opponent disengages as you chase**  
   → **Default:** clamp earlier; if contact breaks, route to distance re-capture.

6) **You have stable containment**  
   → **Default:** proceed to finishing systems or transition to a more dominant hub.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Turn-away/run:** follow rotation; re-seat 50/50; deny hip clearance.
- **Knee-line clear attempt:** re-seat lower; add redundancy; deny one-step clear.
- **Backstep/rotation out:** keep connection; track hip; re-seat.
- **Pull-away:** clamp; if lost, route rather than chase.
- **Hand-fight/strip:** replace grips while preserving leg structure; structure > hands.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“He still ran away.”** → turn-away gate violated; re-seat before attacking.
- **“He cleared knee line fast.”** → knee line not controlled; re-seat lower + redundancy.
- **“I overreached and lost everything.”** → trade-state gate violated; stabilize first.
- **“He disengaged to distance.”** → pull-away gate violated; clamp earlier or route immediately.

## 9. Key Cues

### 9.1 Permission Cues
- Opponent’s main defense is turn-away/run.
- You can establish containment that blocks rotation-based disengage.
- You can deny one-step knee-line clearance.

### 9.2 Abort Cues
- Opponent consistently clears knee line in one action.
- You cannot stop rotation from creating distance.
- Disengage to two steps occurs repeatedly.

### 9.3 Success Landmarks
- Turn-away stalls and does not create distance.
- 3-second stable hold achievable.
- Clear route exists to finish or to a stronger containment hub.

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist

- [ ] knee line not clearing in one action
- [ ] 3-second stable hold available
- [ ] you can transition to double 50 or criss-cross when needed
- [ ] you are not overreaching (trade-state maintained)

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Turn-Away Containment Reps
- **Start:** opponent begins turning away immediately from seated 50/50.
- **Defender wins:** deny turn-away and hold 3s.
- **Passer wins:** create disengage to two steps within 5s.
- **Rules:** no finishing attempts until 3s containment achieved.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Knee-Line Clear Audit
- **Start:** seated 50/50; opponent’s only goal is one-step knee-line clear.
- **Defender wins:** deny clear for 5s (re-seat lower + redundancy).
- **Passer wins:** clear within 5s.
- **Rules:** structure fix only; no hand chasing.
- **Reset:** timer or clear.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Route Decision (50/50 vs double 50 vs criss-cross)
- **Start:** seated 50/50; opponent alternates between slippery clears and backside pushing.
- **Defender wins:** correctly route within 5s to (a) stay 50/50, (b) upgrade to double 50, or (c) shift to criss-cross; then hold 3s.
- **Passer wins:** escape within 5s.
- **Rules:** wrong route choice = rep loss.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.
