# Conversion loop

## Purpose

A repeatable decision loop for converting leg entanglements into **top advantage**:
- back takes when the opponent turns away
- pins/passes when the opponent turns toward
- no resets to neutral after a denied attempt

## Core idea

- Do not treat “leg exchange” as a separate game.
- Treat leg entanglement as an **entry hub** whose job is to force reactions that open:
  - **back attachment**, or
  - **stable pin / passing progression**.

## Key positions

- Starting hubs (examples):
  - irimi ashi
  - outside ashi (forward phase)
  - cross ashi
  - inside ashi / 50-50 (as escape-to-convert hubs)
- Stabilization platforms (common landings):
  - camping
  - north-south pin
  - cross-body ride
  - tight waist
  - body lock / side body lock

## Decision rules (IF/THEN)

- IF you are in a leg entanglement AND your safety gates are satisfied -> THEN begin conversion (do not stall).
- IF the opponent **turns away** under your pressure -> THEN attach chest-to-upper-back (connection before hooks).
- IF the opponent **turns toward** to deny attachment -> THEN accept chest-to-chest and progress passing/pinning.
- IF a backtake attempt is **denied / slipping** -> THEN convert immediately into a pin/passing lane (tight waist / camping / north-south / ride).
- IF a passing sequence produces **turn-away** -> THEN treat it as your back-opening trigger and attach.
- IF you cannot achieve a stable pin OR stable attachment -> THEN stabilize first (camping / north-south / ride), then re-run the fork.

## Reaction triggers (what you are trying to force)

- Turn-away (fleeing pressure) -> opens back attachment
- Turn-toward (facing to defend back) -> concedes pin/passing advancement
- Turtle attempt -> can be captured as it forms (anchor-first), then branched into pass or back

## Failure signatures + fixes

- Failure: you “win the legs” but stall with no progress.
  - Fix: run the fork—either attach the back or enter a stabilization platform to pass.
- Failure: you float between pass/back and the opponent re-squares.
  - Fix: commit to a lane immediately after the reaction; stabilize before advancing.
- Failure: you chase hooks without upper-back connection.
  - Fix: rebuild chest-to-upper-back + centre-line alignment, then insert hooks.
- Failure: you reset to neutral after a denied attempt.
  - Fix: treat denial as a trigger to enter a stabilizer (tight waist / camping / north-south / ride).

## Completion checks

- Every exchange should end in one of:
  - stable back attachment (upper-back connection + alignment)
  - stable pin/passing platform (cranial control present)
  - stabilized intermediate platform that forces the next predictable reaction (camping/north-south/ride)
