# Entries — X-Guard Triple-Attack (Triad of Ashi + Far-Leg/High-Leg Counters)

## 1. Purpose
Use **X-guard** as an entry hub to create a **triad (“triple attack”)** on the legs by chaining three entanglements:
1) **Irimi Ashi** (linear, safe entry that ties opponent up),
2) **Classic X-Guard** (hands-to-mat destabilization),
3) **Reverse X-Guard** (fast conversion to strong leg entanglement).

This system also includes the two practical opponent counters to reverse X-guard entries—**far-leg heavy** and **far-leg high**—and the corresponding solutions that preserve access to legs and deny common escapes (flee/backstep/handfight).

(Ref: TRN Vol 7 ~0:05–13:04)

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- Opponent is standing (often staggered stance) or transitioning through standing.
- You can enter **Irimi Ashi** first to make X-guard entry safe (X-guard is risky if you “reach and lean”).
- You can achieve **classic X-guard structure** (foot in front of hip + foot behind knee) and sit-up posture.
- You can reliably force **hands to the mat** (primary objective in X-guard).
- You can recognize the far-leg counter: **heavy** (weight loading) vs **high** (leg lifted away).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You move through the triad cleanly: **Irimi → X → Reverse X** (when appropriate).
- You consistently get opponent’s **hands down** before you transition.
- You have a correct response to:
  - **far-leg heavy**: shoelace/armpit turn-around-corner to backside exposures + 50/50 variants,
  - **far-leg high**: two-on-one calf/knee + corner turn + Achilles catch + tombstone control to prevent backstep/turn-out.
- You maintain control against major exits: **flee the mat**, **backstep**, **turn-out**, **counter-leglock threat**.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**IRIMI (linear tie-up) → SWITCH TO X-GUARD → HANDS DOWN → CHOOSE ATTACK LANE**
- **Lane A (inexperienced defender):** reverse X-guard lift → easy conversion to leg entanglement.
- **Lane B (far-leg heavy):** shoelace catch + foot to armpit/lat → turn corner → heel exposure → 50/50 variants (belly-down ideal; inside-shoulder follow if late).
- **Lane C (far-leg high):** two-on-one calf/back-knee + turn corner → foot threading + Achilles catch to stop flee → tombstone foot-on-sternum to stop backstep/turn-out → finish layer.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 “No Reach-Into-X” Gate (entry safety)
Direct leaning/reaching into X-guard invites **backstep** and trouble.
- **Default action:** enter **Irimi Ashi first** to tie up legs and create safe contact, then switch to X-guard.

### 5.2 Hands-Down Gate (X-guard invariant)
Before switching to reverse X / corner turns, your priority is opponent’s **hands to the mat**.
- **Default action:** elevate the far leg and extend your legs until hands post.

### 5.3 Counter-ID Gate (choose heavy vs high)
Your follow-up depends on what the opponent does to the far leg after you threaten reverse X.
- **Default action:** classify: **heavy** (weight sinks) vs **high** (leg lifts away) before committing to a finish lane.

### 5.4 Flee/Backstep Gate (containment requirement)
When you lose shoelace-generated “automatic heel exposure” (common in high-leg branch), escapes increase:
- flee risk → solve with **Achilles catch / bicep trap**,
- backstep risk → solve with **tombstone foot control on sternum**.

### 5.5 Defensive Foot Position Gate (counter-leglock hygiene)
When opponent can access your foot/heel during the exchange, you must adopt the defensive foot position and get your knee line safe.
- **Default action:** enter defensive foot position promptly; fight knee inside; clear foot from threat.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Standing opponent in staggered stance; you feel “reach to X” is risky**  
   → **Default:** Irimi Ashi first (linear tie-up) → then switch to X-guard.

2) **You’re in classic X-guard; opponent’s hands are still free**  
   → **Default:** elevate far leg + extend to put hands down (non-negotiable).

3) **Reverse X-guard lift is easy (opponent doesn’t know the leg game)**  
   → **Default:** reverse X and lift hips over head → convert to strong leg entanglement.

4) **Opponent makes the far leg heavy**  
   → **Default:** shoelace catch + foot to armpit/lat → turn corner → heel exposure → feed to 50/50 variants (belly-down ideal; inside-shoulder follow if late).

5) **Opponent makes the far leg high**  
   → **Default:** two-on-one calf/back-knee → turn corner → thread foot; stop flee with Achilles catch; stop backstep/turn-out with tombstone on sternum.

6) **Opponent tries to backstep out of your finishing alignment**  
   → **Default:** do NOT point knees to ceiling; follow to inside-shoulder alignment and keep edge-of-foot near belly button line to track the backstep.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Backstep when you reach for X:** you violated Gate 5.1 → re-enter via Irimi Ashi tie-up.
- **Far leg heavy:** go shoelace/armpit corner-turn lane; generate heel exposure; transition to 50/50 variants.
- **Far leg high:** switch to two-on-one grip; accept reduced heel auto-exposure; immediately add flee/backstep containment.
- **Flee the mat (pull away):** Achilles catch / bicep trap; knees down; clamp connection.
- **Backstep during high-leg lane:** tombstone foot on sternum; keep foot blade centered to prevent toe turn-away.
- **Turn-out / toe rotation:** tombstone denies toe turn; later cross-hand hold maintains center-chest control.
- **Counter leglock threat on your foot:** defensive foot position + knee inside; clear the foot.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I got backstepped entering X-guard.”** → stop reaching; Irimi Ashi first.
- **“I can’t lift into reverse X anymore.”** → opponent is heavy/high; classify and select the correct lane.
- **“He fled the mat in the high-leg branch.”** → you skipped Achilles/bicep catch.
- **“He backstepped and killed my finish.”** → you failed tombstone/center-chest foot control; or you pointed knees to ceiling instead of following to inside-shoulder.
- **“He hand-fought my lock open.”** → you didn’t go to inside-shoulder alignment (harder for them to reach head/elbow).

## 9. Trigger Recognition Checklist
Proceed to the relevant branch when 2+ cues appear:

**Heavy-leg branch**
- [ ] far leg “sinks” (weight loads; lifting feels dead)
- [ ] reverse X lift stalls
- [ ] shoelace access is available to initiate corner turn

**High-leg branch**
- [ ] far leg lifts high (distance increases; no close contact)
- [ ] shoelace manipulation is denied
- [ ] two-on-one calf/back-knee control is available

**Finish-containment branch (either)**
- [ ] flee attempt begins (hip withdrawal / pull-away)
- [ ] backstep attempt begins (hip turns / step behind)
- [ ] toe-turn/turn-out begins

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
Before you consider the sequence “locked”:
- [ ] you entered X-guard via Irimi tie-up (no reach-into-X)
- [ ] opponent’s hands are down before you branch
- [ ] branch choice matches the counter (heavy vs high)
- [ ] flee is denied (Achilles/bicep catch in high-leg branch)
- [ ] backstep is denied (follow to inside-shoulder / tombstone sternum control)
- [ ] your defensive foot position is ready if opponent hunts your foot

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Triad Entry Compliance (Irimi → X safely)
- **Start:** opponent standing staggered; your goal is to enter Irimi then switch to X.
- **Defender wins:** reach classic X-guard without being backstepped; then put hands down (pause 2s).
- **Passer wins:** successful backstep pass OR disengage to two steps.
- **Rules:** any direct “reach into X” attempt is rep loss.
- **Reset:** 2s pause with hands down, or pass/disengage.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Heavy vs High Recognition Branching
- **Start:** you are in classic X with hands down; partner chooses heavy-leg or high-leg on cue.
- **Defender wins:** select correct branch and achieve the first containment landmark:
  - heavy: corner turn + heel exposure,
  - high: two-on-one + Achilles/bicep catch.
- **Passer wins:** you choose wrong branch or stall for 10s.
- **Rules:** you must verbally (to yourself) label “heavy” or “high” before acting.
- **Reset:** landmark achieved or 10s.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Anti-Backstep Foot Control (tombstone/inside-shoulder)
- **Start:** high-leg branch position; opponent’s only job is to backstep/turn-out and handfight.
- **Defender wins:** establish tombstone/center-chest foot control and deny escape for 5s.
- **Passer wins:** successful backstep/turn-out escape within 5s.
- **Rules:** if your knees point to ceiling during backstep, rep loss (must follow to inside-shoulder line).
- **Reset:** 5s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
