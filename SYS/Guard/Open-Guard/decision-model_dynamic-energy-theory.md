# Decision Model — Dynamic Energy Theory (Open Guard)

## 1. Purpose
A fast selection model for open guard that chooses the correct response family based on opponent commitment.

Dynamic Energy answers:
- what direction is the opponent committing force, and
- what does that imply about the highest-percentage counter family right now?

## 2. Core Read (two axes)
Read the opponent on two axes:

### 2.1 Push vs Pull
- **Push:** opponent drives into you / shoves legs / compresses / forces you back
- **Pull:** opponent retracts / strips grips / backsteps / creates separation / yanks away

### 2.2 Forward vs Backward
- **Forward:** weight and intention are coming toward your hip line (closing distance)
- **Backward:** weight and intention are leaving contact (resetting range)

These reads are used to prevent “wrong-family” responses (e.g., trying to spin under when the opponent is stable and pulling away).

## 3. Operational Use (what to do with the read)

### 3.1 If energy is FORWARD + PUSH
- Expect: pressure entry, level change, angle build through contact
- Priorities:
  - protect hip line (hip line gate awareness)
  - build frames/wedges if needed
  - deny distance and level progression early
- Common error: treating this as a mobility problem and chasing with hands while getting compressed

### 3.2 If energy is FORWARD + PULL
- Expect: snap-down style clears, grip strips into re-entry, baiting overreach
- Priorities:
  - maintain redundant attachment (hands + feet)
  - avoid reaching and losing orientation
  - keep them in front; re-square as they try to create angle on the pull
- Common error: overcommitting to a handle that is already being stripped

### 3.3 If energy is BACKWARD + PULL
- Expect: backstep, disengage, flee-the-mat behavior, leg retraction (high leg)
- Priorities:
  - anti-exit governance: follow and keep attachment
  - occupy space to prevent clean disengagement
  - select containment hubs when entangled (50–50/double 50 as needed)
- Common error: accepting the reset and restarting “cold” without connection

### 3.4 If energy is BACKWARD + PUSH
- Expect: shove-to-clear (push legs away then retreat), torreando-style clear attempts
- Priorities:
  - feet-first reconnection; deny angle
  - reinsert legs as barriers immediately
  - prevent the first clean step that removes your frames/hooks
- Common error: staying square and letting them circle outside your knee line

## 4. Validation Tests (did you read correctly?)
Your read is correct if you can predict the next beat:

- **Correct read produces:** forced steps, posts, re-centering, or slowed passing.
- **Wrong read produces:** you reach, lose orientation, and they accumulate angle or cross the hip line.

## 5. Integration with Gates (non-negotiable)
Dynamic Energy does not override the master gates:
- If **hip line is threatened**, defensive cycle is mandatory.
- If **hands-down gate is not achieved**, do not spin/rotate under stable base.
- Maintain **orientation rule** regardless of read.

## 6. Failure Signatures
- “I reacted but nothing changed” → read likely wrong or attachment not redundant
- “He just stepped away” → backward+pull not governed (anti-backstep/flee missing)
- “I spun and got smashed” → hands-down gate violated (stable base)
- “He got past my knees fast” → orientation rule violated during the exchange
