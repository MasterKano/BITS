# Entries — Cross-Catch out of Irimi Ashi (Anti High-Leg / Anti Heavy-Leg Backstep Lace)

## 1. Purpose
When opponent clears your initial Ashi/I­rimi-style connection, two common defenses appear:
- **Heavy leg** (weight loads the far leg so it feels impossible to elevate),
- **High leg** (leg lifts so high you lose access).

This system forces a **cross-catch** that makes both defenses fail by **forcing a backstep** (leg comes close; cannot be high or heavy), then converts to a strong knockdown/control (inside senkaku / “lace leg” finishing position).

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can enter initial **Irimi/Ashi Garami** connection (terminology varies in transcript, but the concept is “entering Ashi”).
- Opponent attempts to clear your controlling foot and/or tries to load weight or lift the leg.
- You can build a **cross frame** and place your foot on opponent’s buttocks to steer.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You force the target leg into a **backstep close to you** (removes high-leg and heavy-leg).
- You catch behind the leg line and lock a control position (inside senkaku / lace leg).
- You can push along the hip line to put opponent down.
- You retain finishing position and/or sweep options.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**IRIMI/ASHI CONNECT → CROSS FRAME → BUTTOCKS POST → OFF-BALANCE OUTSIDE EDGE → FORCE BACKSTEP → CATCH/LACE → PUT DOWN**

Key mechanic: **push along the weakness of the outside edge of the foot** to force a backstep.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Shoelace Coverage Gate
When opponent clears your foot, you must cover/secure the shoelaces region so you don’t lose the connection.
- **Default action:** re-cover the clearing line and set your cross frame before forcing movement.

### 5.2 Anti Heavy-Leg Gate
If the far leg is weighted, direct elevation often fails.
- **Default action:** don’t “lift harder”; force the backstep by pushing over the outside edge.

### 5.3 Anti High-Leg Gate
If the leg is lifting high, pure chasing fails.
- **Default action:** force the backstep first; once leg steps close, high-leg is too late.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Opponent clears your foot early**  
   → **Default:** cover shoelaces + build cross frame.

2) **Opponent loads weight (heavy leg)**  
   → **Default:** force backstep via outside-edge off-balance.

3) **Opponent lifts leg high (high leg)**  
   → **Default:** force backstep first (high-leg becomes impossible).

4) **Opponent tries to flee**  
   → **Default:** lace/catch denies pull-away; put down along hip line.

5) **You achieve inside senkaku / lace**  
   → **Default:** consolidate; sweeping and finishing options exist (finishing later in series).

6) **You want to understand why it works**  
   → **Default:** drill one-leg balance: outside-edge push forces step—this is the entry’s engine.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Clears the foot:** cover shoelaces; cross frame; set buttocks post.
- **Heavy far leg:** off-balance laterally to force backstep.
- **High leg attempt:** backstep forced first; leg comes close; catch.
- **Flee the mat:** lace/catch makes pull-away very difficult; put down.
- **Bases with arms wide:** push along hip line; they still go down.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“He felt like a thousand pounds.”** → you fought weight; should force backstep via outside-edge push.
- **“He high-legged and I lost the leg.”** → you chased; should force backstep first.
- **“I lost the connection when he cleared my foot.”** → failed shoelace coverage; re-cover and frame.

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 One-Leg Outside-Edge Drill (mechanics)
- **Start:** partner balances with weight on one leg (other leg light).
- **Defender wins:** force a backstep by pushing laterally over outside edge (clean step reaction).
- **Passer wins:** maintains balance without stepping for 5s.
- **Rules:** no forward/back pushes; only lateral outside-edge push.
- **Reset:** step or 5s hold.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each side, switch.

### 13.2 Anti High-Leg / Heavy-Leg Game
- **Start:** you have initial Ashi/I­rimi connect; partner’s job is to either heavy-leg or high-leg on cue.
- **Defender wins:** force backstep and secure catch/lace; hold 3s.
- **Passer wins:** clear to disengage or deny backstep for 10s.
- **Rules:** you may not chase the leg upward; backstep must be forced.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or 10s timer.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Put-Down Along Hip Line
- **Start:** catch/lace secured; partner bases with arms wide.
- **Defender wins:** put them down along hip line within 5s.
- **Passer wins:** stays up 5s or clears the leg line.
- **Rules:** prioritize steering along hip line (not muscling).
- **Reset:** put-down or timer.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
