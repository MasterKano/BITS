# Retention Movement — Reverse Shrimp (Back-Out Realignment)

## 1. Purpose
A retention movement system for backing the hips away to realign barriers and re-center when the passer is compressing or starting to step deep.

Reverse shrimp is used when lateral shrimping alone is not enough, or when you need to:
- restore knee line alignment,
- rebuild distance without conceding angle,
- prevent penetration from a deep step.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can recognize early penetration lines (deep step to hip line).
- You can maintain orientation (feet or knees facing) while backing out.
- You can reinsert barriers immediately after creating distance.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You increase hip-to-hip distance (penetration line broken).
- You reinsert the knee/foot barrier line between bodies.
- You re-center (opponent in front) and reduce outside knee line risk.
- You can transition to counter start after cycle break.

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 What Reverse Shrimp Does
- Moves hips backward (away from the passer) to break penetration lines.
- Re-aligns your knee line to face the opponent’s center.
- Buys space for barrier reinsertion without excessive lateral exposure.

### 4.2 Triggers (when to reverse shrimp)
Use reverse shrimp when:
- passer is stepping deep and your hips are getting “captured,”
- you need distance but lateral shrimping is feeding angle,
- pressure is present but not yet a full pin.

If hip line is already fully compromised and flattening is imminent, you likely need stronger structure and defensive cycle sequencing first.

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks
Reverse shrimp primarily denies:
- **Penetration** (hip line access) and
- reduces **Angle** risk created by lateral hip escape.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Hip Line Gate
If hip line is threatened, you are in defensive cycle until cycle break.

**Default action:** frame stack → turn-to-side → reverse shrimp to break penetration line → reinsert barriers → re-square.

### 5.2 Orientation Rule
Reverse shrimp must preserve facing; backing out while turned away creates angle loss.

**Default action:** keep knees facing as you back out; re-square immediately if angle forms.

### 5.3 Hands-Down Gate (commitment)
Reverse shrimp is defensive movement; no committed entries under stable base or active pressure.

**Default action:** reach cycle break first; then counter.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses

1) **Passer follows hips forward (tracks the back-out)**
   - Response: maintain frame stack; repeat reverse shrimp in increments; reinsert barriers as soon as space exists.

2) **Passer circles as you back out**
   - Response: re-square immediately (crossover) so reverse shrimp does not feed outside knee line.

3) **Passer attempts to flatten during the back-out**
   - Response: prioritize anti-flattening structure (high frame); do not sacrifice shoulders; then back out again.

4) **Passer retreats after you recover**
   - Response: scoot-follow; upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle to deny disengage.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **Reverse shrimp creates angle loss** → you backed out while turned; preserve knees facing; add crossover as soon as circling occurs.
- **I back out but can’t reinsert barriers** → you’re moving distance without reinsertion; convert space into knee/foot barriers immediately.
- **I reverse shrimp too late and get pinned** → you missed the penetration trigger; frame earlier at level threat.
- **I keep doing reverse shrimp when it’s a mobility passer** → wrong family; use crossovers/scoot instead.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Deep-Step Denial (back-out timing)
- **Start:** passer begins with a deep step approaching hip line (late stage), not pinned.
- **Defender wins:** reverse shrimp to break penetration line, reinsert barriers, then hold squared guard for **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **hip line touch** sustained or **pin**.
- **Rules:** defender must preserve facing while backing out; reinsertion is mandatory.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch / pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 10.2 Back-Out → Reinsertion Conversion
- **Start:** passer applies moderate pressure; defender’s knees are drifting away from facing.
- **Defender wins:** reverse shrimp to regain distance, then foot/knee barriers reinserted; hold “in front” **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** then **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** if defender backs out without reinsertion, restart rep.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 10.3 Back-Out With Circle Follow
- **Start:** passer pressures forward, then circles as defender backs out.
- **Defender wins:** reverse shrimp, then crossover to deny angle; re-center and hold **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** **3s** or **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** defender must show correct add-on (back-out + crossover).
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.
