# Strategy — Constant Threat (Leg Target)

## 1. Purpose
A strategic operating rule for Open Guard:

**Always maintain a credible leg-line threat** (real enough that the passer must respect it), because persistent leg danger slows passing, forces conservative footwork, and creates repeated windows for re-centering and counter starts.

This is not “attack at all costs.” It is a threat model that stabilizes retention and reduces reset freedom.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can re-establish barriers (legs between bodies) when they are shucked.
- You can upgrade to a **true handle** on the ankle/heel line when distance exists.
- You can maintain orientation (feet or knees facing) while threatening legs.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
A good “constant threat” posture produces one or more:
- passer hesitates to step deep (reduced penetration attempts),
- passer widens base / posts to recover balance (base events increase),
- passer cannot disengage cleanly to two steps (must strip first),
- you repeatedly secure ankle/heel line true handles,
- you create counter starts after cycle break with minimal delay.

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 Threat Definition (what “credible” means)
A leg threat is credible only if:
- you can attach to ankle/heel line (true handle), or
- your legs are positioned to immediately reattach if they step in.

Touching feet without line control is not a threat; it is noise.

### 4.2 Threat Loop (how it supports retention)
**BARRIERS → LEG THREAT → FORCE REACTION → RECENTER → REATTACH**

- barriers keep you safe while you build threat
- threat forces conservative passing and base events
- reactions create re-centering opportunities
- reattachment prevents reset

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks
Constant leg threat primarily denies:
- **Distance** (they cannot step deep freely),
- **Angle** (their circling becomes slower), and
- **Reset** (they must strip the leg line before retreating).

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Hip Line Gate
If hip line is threatened, strategy stops; you must defend.

**Default action:** frames high/low → turn-to-side → shrimp/recover barriers → re-square.

### 5.2 Hands-Down Gate (commitment)
No committed entry/rotation under stable base.

**Default action:** treat threat as base-break first; commit only after post/widen/forced step.

### 5.3 Orientation Rule
Feet or knees face opponent.

**Default action:** re-square hips before upgrading threats.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses
Common passer reactions to constant leg threat:

1) **Retreat/backstep to disengage**
   - Response: follow with hips; keep ankle/heel line; add redundancy.

2) **Shove-to-clear and circle**
   - Response: reinsertion first (foot pummel); then re-square; reattach ankle line.

3) **Drop posture (pressure) to crush threat**
   - Response: frames early; deny level; re-center; reapply leg threat on cycle break.

4) **Grip strip then step**
   - Response: reattach leg line immediately; do not reach; maintain orientation.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **Threat is ignored** → it wasn’t credible; upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle and redundancy.
- **I got passed while attacking legs** → gates violated; hip line threat means defend first.
- **I lose contact on retreat** → follow too late; hips must travel; add redundancy.
- **I chase feet** → line control missing; stop touching, start controlling ankle/heel line.
- **Constant threat becomes constant stalling** → force reactions: off-balance, re-center, reattach; do not hold static grips.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Threat Must Cause a Reaction
- **Start:** passer standing; defender seated open guard.
- **Defender wins:** secure ankle/heel line (true handle) and force either **post/widen/forced step** or prevent **disengage to two steps** for **15s**.
- **Passer wins:** strips contact then **disengages to two steps**, or achieves **outside knee line** then **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** defender may not settle for foot touching; must seek line control.
- **Reset:** timer / disengage / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.2 Anti-Reset With Leg Threat
- **Start:** range with light contact allowed.
- **Defender wins:** maintain any contact for **15s** while attempting to upgrade to ankle/heel line.
- **Passer wins:** **disengage to two steps** with no contact.
- **Rules:** passer must attempt retreat/backstep repeatedly; defender must follow with hips.
- **Reset:** disengage / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.3 Threat Under Pressure (gate compliance)
- **Start:** passer begins with forward pressure (shoulders low).
- **Defender wins:** build structure and achieve cycle break; then begin leg-lane counter start within **2s**.
- **Passer wins:** **pin** or flattened shoulders **3s**.
- **Rules:** defender may not chase legs until cycle break exists.
- **Reset:** counter start / pin / 3s hold.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.
