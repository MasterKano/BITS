# Application — Pressure Passer Defense Ladder (Open Guard)

## 1. Purpose
An application profile for defending a **pressure passer**: someone who advances by compressing posture, dropping level, and progressively removing barriers to reach penetration and pin.

Goal: defend early (deny level), prevent hip line penetration, and force cycle breaks that convert into immediate counter-offense.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can build structure (frames high/low) under compression.
- You can turn-to-side and shrimp to recover barriers.
- You can recognize the pressure ladder level and act before the next level opens.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You prevent progression to the next ladder level.
- You keep shoulders from flattening (no pin consolidation).
- You exit penetration attempts into cycle break.
- You counter within 2 seconds after cycle break (no stop-pass-stop).

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 Pressure Passer (operational definition)
Identified by:
- sustained forward+push intent,
- posture lowering / shoulder pressure,
- incremental barrier removal (not circling away),
- preference for pin consolidation over resets.

Primary threat is **Level → Penetration → Pin**.

### 4.2 Pressure Defense Ladder (levels)
Treat pressure passing as a ladder. Your job is to stop the climb early.

| Level | What it looks like | Primary deny | Primary tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1: Contact | hands/forearms controlling legs; stable base | barrier removal | foot pummel + distance management |
| L2: Level | shoulders drop; chest compress; posture lowers | level/shoulder line | frames high/low + turn-to-side |
| L3: Angle | outside knee line forming under pressure | angle | re-square when possible; structure if not |
| L4: Penetration | hip line threatened; legs cleared near hips | penetration | defensive cycle (turn + shrimp) |
| L5: Pin | shoulders flatten; chest-to-chest consolidation | pin | prevent flattening; rebuild frames; re-guard |

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks (pressure profile)
Pressure passer is building:
- **Level → Penetration → Pin**
So your denial focus is:
- structure early, turn-to-side early, cycle break, then counter.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Hip Line Gate (non-negotiable)
If hip line is threatened, you are in defensive cycle until cycle break.

**Default action:** frames high/low → turn-to-side → shrimp/recover barriers → re-square.

### 5.2 Hands-Down Gate (commitment validity)
No committed rotations/entries under stable base.

**Default action:** base-break first; commit only after post/widen/forced step.

### 5.3 Orientation Rule (integrity)
Feet or knees must face opponent. Under pressure, this becomes “recover facing as part of the cycle.”

**Default action:** turn-to-side with structure, then reinsert barriers to regain facing.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses (Pressure Ladder)

### 6.1 L1 → L2 (contact becoming level)
**Read:** shoulders dropping; forward drive begins.  
**Response:** frames high/low immediately; do not wait for penetration.

### 6.2 L2 → L3 (level creating angle)
**Read:** pressure plus circling; outside knee line forming under weight.  
**Response:** if you can re-square, do it; if not, keep structure and deny the angle with your hips and frames.

### 6.3 L3 → L4 (angle turning into penetration)
**Read:** knee line cleared; hip line threatened.  
**Response:** defensive cycle now (turn-to-side + shrimp); rebuild barriers.

### 6.4 L4 → L5 (penetration into pin)
**Read:** shoulders flattening; chest-to-chest consolidation.  
**Response:** prevent flattening; rebuild frames; recover guard or reinsert legs.

### 6.5 Pressure passer “stall pressure”
**Read:** heavy weight without progress, trying to exhaust you.  
**Response:** structure + micro-mobility; create cycle break and counter rather than accepting static compression.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I got flattened before I framed”** → structure too late; frames must start at L2, not L4.
- **“I tried to re-square and got smashed”** → wrong timing; under L2/L4 pressure, turn-to-side first.
- **“I recovered but he just kept pressuring”** → no counter after cycle break; enforce 2-second rule.
- **“I entered under pressure and got pinned”** → gate violation; no committed entries under stable base/hip line threat.
- **“I can’t stop the ladder”** → identify which level you’re losing; drill that transition.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Pressure Ladder Rounds
- **Start:** passer standing or kneeling in front; defender open guard.
- **Defender wins:** prevent **hip line touch** and avoid **pin** for **20s**.
- **Passer wins:** **hip line touch** or **pin**.
- **Rules:** passer must apply sustained forward pressure (no circling away to reset).
- **Reset:** hip line touch / pin / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.2 Level Denial (L2 focus)
- **Start:** passer begins with shoulders already low and hands controlling legs.
- **Defender wins:** build frames and turn-to-side, then re-center for **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** progress to **hip line touch** or **pin**.
- **Rules:** defender must frame immediately; no committed entries.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch / pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 10.3 Penetration Escape (L4 focus)
- **Start:** passer begins near hip line (late stage), not pinned.
- **Defender wins:** achieve cycle break and hold squared guard for **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **pin** or flattened shoulders **3s**.
- **Rules:** defender must use defensive cycle tools (turn + shrimp) until squared.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 10.4 Cycle Break → Counter (pressure profile)
- **Start:** passer begins mid-pressure; defender must recover to cycle break.
- **Defender wins:** cycle break then start counter within **2s**.
- **Passer wins:** **pin** or maintain pressure to immediate re-penetration.
- **Rules:** if defender pauses after recovery, rep is a loss.
- **Reset:** counter start / pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.
