# Application — Pressure Passer (Defense Ladder)

## 1. Purpose
A structured escalation ladder for pressure passers whose primary weapon is **distance collapse → level advantage → penetration → pin** via compression, locks, and stabilization.

## 2. Identification (pressure passer signals)
- closes distance to compress hips/shoulders
- seeks stable attachments (body lock, underhooks, clamps, head position)
- less circling; more drive-in and stabilization
- passing feels like “being pinned in stages”

## 3. Threat Model (what you must stop)
Pressure passing succeeds by:
- collapsing distance,
- winning level and posture,
- controlling knee line/hip line,
- then pinning shoulder/hip line so guard cannot be rebuilt.

## 4. Defense Ladder (in-order escalation)
### 4.1 Stage 1 — Deny lock formation (early)
- do not allow stable attachments to form
- rebuild inside barriers early
- if you win here, you never enter late defense

### 4.2 Stage 2 — Wedges + Frames (structure, not pushing)
- once compression begins, frames become structural
- choose frame high vs frame low based on what is being threatened (shoulder line vs hip line)

### 4.3 Stage 3 — Space creation (movement under structure)
- combine frame with shrimp/reverse shrimp to create space
- objective is not “push away”; objective is “space to reinsert legs”

### 4.4 Stage 4 — Barrier reinsertion (guard restoration)
- legs must be reinserted between bodies
- re-square and re-center opponent

### 4.5 Stage 5 — Counter on cycle break
- do not pause; immediate counter-offense (golden rule selector)

## 5. Gates (mandatory)
- Hip line threatened → defensive cycle (frames + structure) is mandatory.
- Shoulder line threatened (pin forming) → treat as late defense; de-pin first, then rebuild.

## 6. Failure Signatures
- “I tried to move but couldn’t” → lock/level already won; you skipped Stage 1
- “I framed but got smashed anyway” → frames were pushing, not structuring; or chosen wrong (high vs low)
- “I made space but it didn’t matter” → you did not reinsert barriers
- “I survived then got passed next beat” → counter-offense mandate not applied
