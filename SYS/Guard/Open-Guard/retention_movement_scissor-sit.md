# Retention Movement — Scissor Sit (Seated Re-Orientation Pivot)

## 1. Purpose
A retention movement system for rapidly pivoting the seated base to keep the opponent in front.

Scissor sit is used when the passer is circling and your hips must rotate to restore:
- orientation (feet/knees facing),
- inside barrier alignment,
- angle denial (outside knee line prevention).

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You are seated or semi-seated (not fully pinned).
- You can post lightly or balance through hips to pivot.
- You can reinsert barriers immediately after the pivot.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- Opponent returns in front within 1–2 beats.
- Outside knee line is denied or corrected.
- Barriers are restored between bodies.
- You can attach to leg line if the passer attempts to reset.

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 What Scissor Sit Does
- Rotates your seated base so your knees/feet face the opponent’s center.
- Resets your knee line alignment without needing full crossover travel.
- Creates a platform for immediate reinsertion (foot pummel) or reattachment.

### 4.2 Triggers (when to scissor sit)
Use scissor sit when:
- you are seated and the passer is circling around your hip line,
- a full crossover would be slow or space-limited,
- you need a quick pivot to stop the corner forming.

If hip line is actively threatened under heavy pressure, you likely need structure and defensive cycle tools instead.

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks
Scissor sit primarily denies:
- **Angle** (outside knee line) by restoring facing and knee line alignment.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Orientation Rule (primary)
Feet or knees must face opponent.

**Default action:** pivot immediately as the corner forms; do not wait for outside knee line to be established.

### 5.2 Hip Line Gate
If hip line is threatened (penetration), switch to defensive cycle.

**Default action:** frames high/low → turn-to-side → shrimp/recover barriers → re-square.

### 5.3 Hands-Down Gate (commitment)
Scissor sit is recovery; it is not a trigger to commit to entries under stable base.

**Default action:** re-center first; commit only after base event.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses

1) **Circle acceleration (runaround)**
   - Response: scissor sit pivot to face, then immediate reinsertion to block the next step.

2) **Circle + shove-to-clear**
   - Response: pivot to face, then foot pummel reinsertion.

3) **Circle then retreat/backstep**
   - Response: pivot to face, then scoot-follow; upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle.

4) **Pressure drop during pivot**
   - Response: frame stack appears; turn-to-side; convert to defensive cycle.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **I pivot but still lose outside knee line** → pivot timing too late; trigger earlier at “corner forming.”
- **Pivot works but my legs aren’t back inside** → reinsertion missing; add foot pummel immediately after pivot.
- **I scissor sit into pressure and get crushed** → misread; pressure requires frames first.
- **I over-rotate and lose facing** → pivot must finish with knees facing; stop when centered.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Seated Corner Denial
- **Start:** defender seated; passer standing and circling.
- **Defender wins:** scissor sit pivot to keep passer in front for **20s** (no outside knee line).
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** then **hip line touch** or **pin**.
- **Rules:** defender must stay seated (no standing); primary tool is scissor sit pivot.
- **Reset:** hip line touch / pin / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.2 Pivot → Reinsertion Combo
- **Start:** passer circles then performs a shove-to-clear once per round.
- **Defender wins:** scissor sit pivot to face, then foot pummel reinsertion; hold “in front” **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** **3s** or **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** mandatory sequence: pivot first, reinsertion second.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.3 Pivot → Follow Reset
- **Start:** passer circles then retreats/backsteps.
- **Defender wins:** pivot to face, then scoot-follow to deny **disengage to two steps** for **15s**.
- **Passer wins:** **disengage to two steps** with no contact.
- **Rules:** defender must pivot first (re-center) before following.
- **Reset:** disengage / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.
