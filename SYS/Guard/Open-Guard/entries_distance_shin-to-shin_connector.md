# Entries (Distance) — Shin-to-Shin Connector (Mid-Range Hub)

## 1. Purpose
A mid-range connector system that links distance control (ankle/leg line) into stable engagement where you can:
- deny resets,
- create base events,
- access deeper entries with less risk than inversion.

Shin-to-shin is treated here as a **connector**, not a destination.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You have a reachable leg line (often via 2-on-1 ankle or after reinsertion).
- You can keep orientation (feet or knees facing).
- You can keep at least one barrier active while building the connector.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You establish shin-to-shin contact that survives one opponent step.
- Opponent remains in front (outside knee line denied).
- You can either (a) upgrade leg-line control, or (b) force a base event, or (c) deny disengage.
- You do not concede hip line threat while connecting.

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 What Shin-to-Shin Connector Does
- Collapses “empty space” between you and the opponent’s lead leg.
- Creates a stable line to prevent free backsteps and lateral runarounds.
- Provides a platform to transition into other systems (distance breaker, kneeling entries, sustained leg threat).

### 4.2 Triggers (when to use shin-to-shin)
Use shin-to-shin when:
- you have leg access but the opponent is retreating/circling too freely,
- you need a mid-range anchor without committing to inversion,
- you want to maintain constant threat while preserving safety.

If pressure is already compressing you and hip line is threatened, connectors are suspended; defend first.

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks
Shin-to-shin primarily denies:
- **Reset** (retreat/backstep) by anchoring the lead leg, and
- reduces **Angle** by limiting lateral freedom.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Hip Line Gate
If hip line is threatened, connector building stops; defensive cycle is required.

**Default action:** frames high/low → turn-to-side → shrimp/recover barriers → re-square → reconnect at mid-range.

### 5.2 Hands-Down Gate (commitment)
Shin-to-shin is a low-risk connector; do not convert it into high-risk rotation under stable base unless a base event exists.

**Default action:** use shin-to-shin to force base event; commit only after post/widen/forced step.

### 5.3 Orientation Rule
Feet or knees must face opponent during connector build.

**Default action:** re-square hips before upgrading; do not build shin-to-shin while turned away.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses (Connector Maintenance)

1) **Backstep / retreat**
   - Response: keep shin-to-shin contact; scoot-follow; upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle.

2) **Circle to outside knee line**
   - Response: maintain connector while re-squaring (crossover/pendulum); do not let the connector pull you sideways.

3) **Shove-to-clear your legs**
   - Response: reinsertion (foot pummel) while maintaining at least one connection; then reestablish shin-to-shin.

4) **Pressure drop to crush**
   - Response: frame stack appears; if hip line threatened, defensive cycle; reconnect after cycle break.

5) **Grip strip attempts**
   - Response: restore redundancy (hand+leg connection); reattach immediately; do not reach.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **Shin-to-shin feels “loose” and he steps away** → you didn’t restrict exit; add follow (scoot) and upgrade to true handle.
- **Connector pulls me out of square** → orientation violation; hips must face; re-square before pulling.
- **I build connector but get crushed** → misread pressure; connector building stops when level/penetration appears.
- **I lose angle while connected** → you kept connection but didn’t re-square; add crossover sooner.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Connector Survives a Step
- **Start:** defender begins with shin-to-shin contact established.
- **Defender wins:** maintain shin-to-shin through one full opponent step and keep opponent in front for **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** breaks connector and **disengages to two steps**, or achieves **outside knee line** then **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** defender must keep hips facing while maintaining contact; no committed rotations.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / disengage / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.2 Retreat Denial (connector to handle)
- **Start:** shin-to-shin established; passer attempts repeated backsteps.
- **Defender wins:** deny disengage to two steps for **15s** and upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle at least once.
- **Passer wins:** disengage to two steps with no contact.
- **Rules:** defender must scoot-follow; “stationary gripping” loses reps.
- **Reset:** timer / disengage.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.3 Connector While Re-Squaring (circle)
- **Start:** passer circles aggressively; defender begins with shin-to-shin.
- **Defender wins:** keep shin-to-shin and deny outside knee line for **15s**.
- **Passer wins:** outside knee line then hip line touch, or pin.
- **Rules:** defender must use crossover/pendulum while maintaining the connector.
- **Reset:** hip line touch / pin / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.
