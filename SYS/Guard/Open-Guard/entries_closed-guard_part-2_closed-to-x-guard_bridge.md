# Entries (Closed Guard) — Part 2: Closed-to-X-Guard Bridge (When They Open, You Convert)

## 1. Purpose
Closed guard in this system is not a terminal position; it is a **bridge**. When the opponent opens or tries to stand to open, you convert directly into **X-guard** (and/or adjacent ashi entries) rather than fighting a losing “re-close” battle.

Core invariant:
**When they open to stand, you enter under and capture legs (X-guard), not chase re-closure.**

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You have achieved a legal closed guard (two-leg control requirement satisfied previously).
- Opponent begins to open via:
  - standing to posture,
  - prying/knee wedge,
  - backstep-style separation,
  - or pull-away/disengage attempt.
- You can keep hip line safe while transitioning (frames/angle available).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- Opponent’s opening attempt becomes your entry trigger.
- You transition to an X-guard-relevant leg capture state where:
  - the opponent cannot simply step free in one motion,
  - you can off-balance immediately,
  - you can route to X-guard attack lanes (Vol 7–8 linkages).
- You avoid chasing re-closure and avoid being flattened during opening.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**CLOSED GUARD → OPENING TRIGGER → UNDER-ENTRY → LEG CAPTURE → X-GUARD → ROUTE**

Operational loop:
1) Detect opening trigger.
2) Insert frames/angle so you can go under safely.
3) Convert to leg capture (deny step-clear).
4) Establish X-guard.
5) Route to X-guard triple attack / momentum entry / hub selection.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 “No Re-Close Fight” Gate
If opponent has committed to opening/standing, chasing re-closure is low percentage.
- **Default action:** treat opening as the trigger to enter under to X-guard.

### 5.2 Hip Line Safety Gate (no under-entry while being collapsed)
If their opening attempt includes pressure collapse, you must frame first.
- **Default action:** frame high/low + turn-to-side + shrimp → create space → under-entry.

### 5.3 Leg Capture Integrity Gate (deny one-step clear)
If your capture allows one-step clear, the bridge fails.
- **Default action:** secure two points of control on leg line before committing to X.

### 5.4 Backstep/Pull-Away Gate (escape pairing)
Opening often pairs with backstep or pull-away.
- **Default action:** follow foot and clamp; if contact breaks, route to distance systems.

### 5.5 Routing Gate (backstep branch)
If the opening attempt converts into a backstep, do not continue the bridge linearly.
- **Default action:** route immediately to anti-backstep governance; then re-enter the bridge once distance is governed.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Opponent stands to open**  
   → **Default:** under-entry immediately; capture legs; establish X.

2) **Opponent wedges knee/pries open**  
   → **Default:** frame + angle; go under rather than re-closing.

3) **Opponent opens then backsteps**  
   → **Default:** follow foot; keep leg capture centered; convert to X or to distance connector if needed.

4) **Opponent opens then pulls away**  
   → **Default:** clamp first; if contact breaks, route to shin-to-shin / 2-on-1 ankle.

5) **Opponent collapses pressure while opening**  
   → **Default:** hip line safety first; frames and shrimp; then under-entry.

6) **You lose capture mid-bridge**  
   → **Default:** do not chase re-close; re-enter via distance connectors.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Stand tall:** go under; capture legs; X-guard.
- **Backstep:** track stepping foot; maintain capture; do not freeze.
- **Pull-away/disengage:** clamp or route; no hand chasing.
- **Pressure collapse:** frames first; then re-enter.
- **Knee-close/leg closure:** avoid forcing; under-entry when lane opens.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I kept trying to re-close and got opened/passed.”** → violated No Re-Close Gate; bridge to X instead.
- **“I went under and he stepped out.”** → capture integrity failed; add redundancy and deny one-step clear.
- **“He backstepped during my bridge.”** → distance governance missing; follow foot and keep capture centered (or route to anti-backstep immediately).
- **“I got flattened during the transition.”** → hip line safety violated; frames first.

## 9. Key Cues

### 9.1 Opening Triggers (go-under permission cues)
- Opponent’s hips rise above your hips (stand-tall intent).
- Their knee line clears your thigh line (separation is real).
- Their hands commit to your body/hips to pry (arms committed; weight shifts).
- One knee wedges in and their base widens (step exists to capture).

### 9.2 “Bad Bridge” Red Flags (abort cues)
- Your shoulders are flat and they are achieving chest-to-chest pressure.
- Your hips are pinned and you cannot angle off.
- You have only one leg captured and no true handle; they can one-step clear.
- Their head is low and driving (pressure consolidation imminent).

### 9.3 Capture Landmarks (success cues)
- You have **two points of control** before going under (leg line + clamp/secondary point).
- Their stepping foot cannot freely move (distance is governed).
- Your entry angle is **under the hips**, not reaching at the knee line.

## 10. Trigger Recognition Checklist
Bridge to X when 2+ cues occur:
- [ ] opponent opens the legs / breaks your lock
- [ ] opponent’s posture rises (stand intent)
- [ ] knee wedge/pry appears
- [ ] you can create a gap with frames/angle
- [ ] you can capture a leg line with at least one hard anchor

## 11. Quality / Capture Checklist
- [ ] you did not chase re-closure
- [ ] hip line was protected during under-entry
- [ ] capture denies one-step clear
- [ ] you arrive at X-guard or an immediate X-adjacent state
- [ ] you can route instantly to a known X lane

## 14. Drills and Games

### 14.1 Opening = Trigger (no re-close)
- **Start:** closed guard; opponent’s only job is to open/stand.
- **Defender wins:** convert to under-entry and establish X-guard within 5s.
- **Passer wins:** open and disengage to two steps or flatten/pin within 5s.
- **Rules:** any attempt to re-close after opening begins is rep loss.
- **Reset:** X established or escape/pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 14.2 Capture Integrity (deny one-step clear)
- **Start:** mid-bridge; defender has partial leg capture; opponent tries one-step clear.
- **Defender wins:** secure redundancy and deny one-step clear for 3s; then establish X.
- **Passer wins:** one-step clear and disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** defender must add a second control point before committing to X.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 14.3 Backstep on Open (track and stay attached)
- **Start:** opponent opens then immediately backsteps.
- **Defender wins:** follow foot and keep capture centered; establish X or distance connector within 5s.
- **Passer wins:** backstep escape/disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** freezing during backstep is rep loss.
- **Reset:** establish X/connector or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
