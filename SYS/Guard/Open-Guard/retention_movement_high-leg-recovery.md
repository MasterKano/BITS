# Retention Movement — High Leg Recovery (Barrier Rebuild Under Angle)

## 1. Purpose
A retention movement system for recovering a **high barrier** when the passer is winning angle and attempting to step deep.

High leg recovery is used to:
- block the passer’s path to your hips,
- interrupt angle → penetration progression,
- restore a functional barrier line that enables re-centering.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You are not fully pinned.
- You can elevate one leg high enough to re-establish a barrier.
- You can re-square hips and reinsert the second barrier after the high leg is set.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- The passer’s direct step line to the hip line is blocked.
- Outside knee line progression is slowed or reversed (opponent returns in front).
- You rebuild a two-barrier system (high barrier + inside reinsertion).
- You can attach to ankle/heel line if passer attempts to reset.

## 4. Core Model / Engine

### 4.1 What High Leg Recovery Does
- Creates a high “stop sign” barrier that prevents deep stepping and compressive hip entry.
- Buys time to re-square hips and rebuild the lower barrier line (foot pummel).
- Converts a losing corner into a recoverable square position.

### 4.2 Triggers (when to use high leg recovery)
Use high leg recovery when:
- passer is circling and you are late on re-square,
- passer is stepping in and your lower barriers are compromised,
- you need a fast “block” before executing crossover/pendulum.

If heavy pressure is already flattening you, prioritize frame stack and defensive cycle.

### 4.3 Domain Progression Chain Hooks
High leg recovery primarily denies:
- **Penetration** (deep step to hip line) and slows
- **Angle** (outside knee line) by blocking the step path.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Orientation Rule (primary)
High leg recovery must preserve or restore facing.

**Default action:** set high barrier while turning hips to face; do not high-leg while turned away.

### 5.2 Hip Line Gate
If hip line is already threatened (deep step established), shift to defensive cycle.

**Default action:** frames high/low → turn-to-side → shrimp/recover barriers → re-square.

### 5.3 Hands-Down Gate (commitment)
High leg recovery is defensive rebuilding; do not convert it into a risky entry under stable base.

**Default action:** re-center first; commit only after base event.

## 6. Opponent Reactions → Responses

1) **Passer tries to shove the high leg down**
   - Response: reinforce with hip angle and re-square; reinsert the second barrier immediately.

2) **Passer circles around the high leg**
   - Response: crossover/scoot to keep in front; treat high leg as a stop-gap, not the finish.

3) **Passer retreats after barrier appears**
   - Response: follow with hips; upgrade to ankle/heel line true handle.

4) **Passer drops into pressure under the high leg**
   - Response: frame stack appears; turn-to-side; convert to defensive cycle.

## 7. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **High leg appears but I still lose angle** → hips didn’t re-square; add crossover immediately after barrier is set.
- **High leg gets stuffed and I collapse** → you waited too long; set earlier or use frames if pressure is already present.
- **I set the high leg and stop moving** → missing follow-up; high leg is a temporary block; reinsertion and re-center must follow.
- **I high-leg while turned away** → orientation violation; fix facing first.

## 10. Drills and Games (Game Cards)

### 10.1 Deep-Step Block (high leg timing)
- **Start:** passer begins near outside knee line and is stepping toward the hip line.
- **Defender wins:** set high leg barrier, re-square, and hold passer in front for **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **hip line touch** or **pin**.
- **Rules:** defender must use high leg recovery as first response; no committed entries.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch / pin.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 10.2 High Barrier → Reinsertion Combo
- **Start:** passer circles and can shove legs aside once per rep.
- **Defender wins:** set high leg barrier, then foot pummel reinsertion; hold “in front” **3s**.
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** **3s** or **hip line touch**.
- **Rules:** mandatory sequence: high barrier first, reinsertion second.
- **Reset:** 3s hold / hip line touch.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.

### 10.3 High Leg Under Mobility Pressure
- **Start:** passer circles aggressively; occasional shove-to-clear allowed.
- **Defender wins:** keep passer in front for **20s** using high leg recovery as needed.
- **Passer wins:** **outside knee line** then **hip line touch** or **pin**.
- **Rules:** passer must keep moving; defender must demonstrate high leg as a recovery tool, not a static hold.
- **Reset:** hip line touch / pin / timer.
- **Rounds:** 8×20s, switch.
