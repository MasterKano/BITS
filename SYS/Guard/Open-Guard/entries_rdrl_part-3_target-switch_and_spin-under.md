# Entries (RDLR) — Part 3: Target Switch + Spin-Under (Re-aim the Control, Don’t “Lose the Platform”)

## 1. Purpose
When RDLR’s base-break platform is established but the opponent’s stance/rotation denies your first intended finish lane, you do not “restart”—you **target switch** (change which leg/line you’re attacking) and/or **spin-under** (re-angle underneath) while preserving:
- calf control integrity,
- distance governance,
- anti-backstep containment.

This is the bridging module that prevents stalled RDLR entries.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You are in RDLR contact with functional control (hook + calf clamp).
- Opponent has reacted to deny your first lane by:
  - rotating hips,
  - changing stance depth,
  - hiding heel line,
  - starting backstep/pull-away sequences.
- You can maintain at least one hard anchor (calf clamp or wedge-in-front) during the switch/spin.

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You re-aim to the correct leg/line without losing the platform.
- You regain a lock state that produces:
  - heel exposure pathway, or
  - multi-leg containment (double 50 / 50-50), or
  - backside compromise stability (criss-cross).
- You deny “escape by distance” during the transition.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**PLATFORM ESTABLISHED → OPPONENT DENIES LANE → CHOOSE: TARGET SWITCH or SPIN-UNDER → RE-LOCK → ROUTE**

Two tools:

### 4.1 Target Switch (re-aim)
Change which leg/line you’re controlling *while keeping the platform*.
- Maintain calf clamp depth.
- Use angle and foot placement to redirect the opponent’s base so the new target cannot step-clear.

### 4.2 Spin-Under (re-angle)
Rotate your hips underneath to re-align their knee/hip line across your hip (reap-direction) and rebuild the pummel lane.
- The goal is not “more inversion”; it’s a re-alignment that makes pummel/lock inevitable.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Anchor Preservation Gate (no empty switching)
You do not switch targets while both anchors are loose.
- **Default action:** keep calf clamp (wrist depth) OR keep center-line foot distance governance before switching.

### 5.2 Backstep Detection Gate (time-critical)
If backstep begins, you must govern distance immediately before any spin/switch.
- **Default action:** foot to **center line** (stomach/chest line) → follow foot → then execute switch/spin.

### 5.3 Knees-Closing Gate (pummel denial)
If knees can close, your pummel/spin is blocked.
- **Default action:** post with second hand to prevent knees closing; then spin/switch.

### 5.4 “No Straight-Leg Throw” Gate (mechanics)
If their target leg is straight, direct pummel attempts fail.
- **Default action:** re-angle (spin-under) to point knee across hip before pummel.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **You have base-break but heel line disappears (they rotate/hide)**  
   → **Default:** target switch to the available leg line without releasing calf clamp.

2) **They step deep / change stance to deny pummel lane**  
   → **Default:** spin-under to re-align knee across hip; rebuild pummel.

3) **Backstep begins during transition**  
   → **Default:** center-line foot first; then switch/spin.

4) **They pull away to disengage**  
   → **Default:** lock posture (knee-to-floor / clamp) before switching target.

5) **They try to close knees**  
   → **Default:** second-hand post to keep knees apart; then execute.

6) **You feel “stalled” but still have a clamp**  
   → **Default:** do not reset; run a switch/spin cycle until a hub lane appears.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Hip rotation hides heel:** target switch; re-aim to accessible leg line; re-lock.
- **Stance change kills pummel:** spin-under; point knee across hip; then pummel/lock.
- **Backstep:** center-line foot; follow; then switch/spin.
- **Pull-away:** drop knee/lock and keep clamp; then switch.
- **Knees close:** post to prevent closure; then execute.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I switched and lost everything.”** → you violated Anchor Preservation; keep clamp/center-line foot first.
- **“He backstepped and I got left behind.”** → you delayed distance governance; center-line foot immediately.
- **“My spin-under did nothing.”** → you didn’t re-align knee across hip; spin must change knee orientation before pummel.
- **“Knees closed and I stalled.”** → you skipped the second-hand post; prevent closure first.

## 9. Trigger Recognition Checklist
Run target switch / spin-under when 2+ cues are true:
- [ ] you have at least one hard anchor (calf clamp or wedge/center-line foot)
- [ ] opponent rotates to hide heel line
- [ ] opponent changes stance depth to deny pummel
- [ ] backstep begins (hip turn + step behind)
- [ ] pull-away begins (hip withdrawal / distance increase)

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
- [ ] calf clamp is wrist-deep (not fingers)
- [ ] you never lose both anchors simultaneously
- [ ] backstep is governed (center-line foot) before re-aiming
- [ ] knees closing is denied before pummel attempts
- [ ] switch/spin ends in a **lock state** (not a reach state)
- [ ] you can route to a hub immediately after the re-lock

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Anchor-Preserved Switching
- **Start:** RDLR hook + calf clamp; opponent rotates to “hide” your lane on cue.
- **Defender wins:** target switch without losing calf clamp; re-lock and hold 3s.
- **Passer wins:** disengage to two steps or break clamp before 3s.
- **Rules:** if you release clamp completely during switch, rep loss.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or disengage.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Backstep-First Governance
- **Start:** platform established; opponent initiates backstep immediately.
- **Defender wins:** center-line foot distance governance + follow foot; then execute switch/spin to a re-lock within 5s.
- **Passer wins:** complete backstep escape within 5s.
- **Rules:** any spin/switch before center-line foot is placed = rep loss.
- **Reset:** re-lock or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Spin-Under Re-Alignment (knee across hip)
- **Start:** opponent keeps target leg straight to deny pummel; you have clamp.
- **Defender wins:** spin-under to re-align knee across hip and achieve pummel landmark (pause 2s) within 10s.
- **Passer wins:** prevent knee across hip for 10s.
- **Rules:** no “throw leg over straight leg” attempts; must re-align first.
- **Reset:** 2s pause or timer.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
