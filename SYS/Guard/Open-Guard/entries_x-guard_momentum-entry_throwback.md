# Entries — X-Guard Momentum Entry (Knee-Down “Throwback”)

## 1. Purpose
A practical X-guard-to-legs entry that uses **momentum**, not strength, to put the opponent’s **hips directly over your head** and enter strong leg positions. Core concept: **put the far-leg knee down**, sit up to load your body weight, then **throw your body backward** (“throwback”) to launch opponent’s hips over you into a leglock-viable configuration.

This is presented as a reliable method to attack **both legs** and to handle opponents who scramble and make static leg entries difficult.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You can reach X-guard safely (typically via Irimi/Ashi first; do not lean-reach into X).
- You can obtain control on the opponent’s **far leg** sufficient to:
  - move your foot to their **shoelaces**,
  - lift their foot,
  - and **put their knee down to the mat**.
- You can sit up without being flattened immediately (frames/angle available if needed).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- Opponent’s **knee is down** and their mobility is reduced.
- You successfully “throwback” so their **hips travel over your head**.
- You establish a usable leg entry state:
  - knee line access stabilized,
  - ability to turn the corner,
  - immediate routing to backside/inside-shoulder control if they backstep or handfight.
- You remain defensively responsible (no exposed foot for counter-control).

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**X-GUARD CONTROL → FOOT TO SHOELACES → KNEE DOWN → SIT UP (load) → THROWBACK (momentum) → HIPS OVER HEAD → STABILIZE → ROUTE**

Mechanics as described:
- Move the controlling foot from “in front” down to **shoelaces**.
- Lift the foot and **put the knee on the mat**.
- Sit up so your back is “off the mat” (you can now add bodyweight).
- Reconfigure legs into a **reverse hexagon** shape.
- **Throw** (not “fall”) your body backward to generate momentum.
- Insert knee behind the opponent’s knee line as the hips travel over.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Entry Safety Gate (no reach-into-X)
If you’re leaning/reaching into X-guard, you invite backstep.
- **Default action:** enter via Irimi/Ashi tie-up, then transition into X.

### 5.2 Knee-Down Gate (momentum prerequisite)
The “throwback” is far higher percentage when the opponent’s **knee is on the mat**.
- **Default action:** shoelaces control → lift foot → place knee down first; do not throwback without knee-down.

### 5.3 “Throw” vs “Fall” Gate (execution)
A passive fall often fails to move opponent’s hips.
- **Default action:** sit up to load weight, then *actively throw* your body backward.

### 5.4 Anti-Backstep Mobility Gate (follow the foot)
If opponent backsteps, you cannot remain stationary (especially not with knee pointing to ceiling).
- **Default action:** your **foot follows his**; keep foot controlled in the **center of the chest** and track the backstep.

### 5.5 Defensive Responsibility Gate (counter-control)
If opponent goes after your foot for counter-control, you must lock your feet defensively.
- **Default action:** lock feet (double-shoelaces / tight foot lock) before continuing to inside-shoulder.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **You have X-guard but opponent is stable and hard to elevate**  
   → **Default:** move foot to shoelaces, lift foot, put knee down.

2) **Knee is down and you can sit up**  
   → **Default:** reverse hexagon + throwback to put hips over head.

3) **Opponent tries to flee/disengage during the sequence**  
   → **Default:** knee-down reduces flee; maintain shoelace control and continue corner-turn mechanics.

4) **Opponent stays and pushes at your feet/hands (naive reaction)**  
   → **Default:** you gain time to transfer and progress to backside/inside-shoulder alignment.

5) **Opponent backsteps (correct reaction)**  
   → **Default:** follow foot; keep foot centered on chest; do not freeze with knee pointing up.

6) **Opponent attacks your exposed foot (counter-control attempt)**  
   → **Default:** lock feet defensively (double-shoelaces) then continue to inside-shoulder.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Resists elevation (heavy/high far leg):** switch to shoelaces, put knee down, use throwback momentum rather than lift strength.
- **Flee attempt:** knee-down + corner-turn mechanics deny pull-away (very difficult).
- **Backstep:** follow the foot; keep it centered on chest; transition to inside-shoulder line.
- **Handfight forward:** their forward motion helps you unlock/advance to inside-shoulder control (do not fear the handfight—use it).
- **Counter-control on your foot:** lock feet defensively before continuing.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I couldn’t get hips over my head.”** → you didn’t sit up/load or you fell instead of throwing; enforce Knee-Down + Throw gate.
- **“I got backstepped and lost everything.”** → you froze; follow the foot and keep it centered on chest.
- **“He countered my foot.”** → defensive responsibility missed; lock feet first (double-shoelaces).
- **“I tried from a loose reach-into-X entry.”** → wrong entry; use Irimi/Ashi to enter X safely.

## 9. Trigger Recognition Checklist
Proceed to throwback when 2+ cues are true:
- [ ] your foot is on **shoelaces** with lift leverage
- [ ] opponent’s **knee is on the mat**
- [ ] you can **sit up** (back comes off mat) without being flattened
- [ ] reverse hexagon leg shape is available
- [ ] opponent’s hips are aligned so “over-head” travel is plausible

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
Before advancing past the throwback:
- [ ] knee-down is maintained (they can’t immediately re-stand)
- [ ] hips travel **over your head** (not sideways collapse)
- [ ] you insert knee behind their knee line as you land
- [ ] if they backstep, your foot follows and stays centered on chest
- [ ] if they attack your foot, you lock feet defensively immediately

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Knee-Down + Throwback Entry (mechanics)
- **Start:** classic X-guard; opponent standing staggered.
- **Defender wins:** foot to shoelaces → knee down → sit up → throwback; demonstrate hips-over-head and knee-behind-knee-line (pause 2s).
- **Passer wins:** prevent knee-down for 10s or force you flat before sit-up.
- **Rules:** no throwback allowed until knee is on mat.
- **Reset:** 2s pause or 10s timer.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Backstep Reaction (follow-foot governance)
- **Start:** after throwback landing; opponent’s job is immediate backstep.
- **Defender wins:** follow foot and keep it centered on chest; deny clear escape for 5s.
- **Passer wins:** clear the foot/line and disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** if your knee points to ceiling while you freeze, rep loss (must track).
- **Reset:** 5s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Counter-Foot Threat (defensive responsibility)
- **Start:** post-throwback; opponent immediately attacks your exposed foot for counter-control.
- **Defender wins:** lock feet defensively (double-shoelaces) and maintain control 3s.
- **Passer wins:** secure counter-control and force disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** you must lock defensively before advancing position.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or 5s timer.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
