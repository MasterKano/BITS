# Entries (RDLR) — Part 4: Leg-Closure + Fill-Ankle-Space (Primary Defence to the Platform)

## 1. Purpose
Define and defeat the opponent’s primary “platform killer” defense against RDLR progressions: **leg closure** combined with **filling the ankle space**. This defense prevents:
- the foot pummel lane,
- the knee-across-hip alignment,
- and the automatic heel exposure sequence.

This page provides the recognition cues, the corrective actions, and the routing rules so RDLR remains usable under a competent defensive opponent.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You have established RDLR contact (hook + calf clamp and/or wedge).
- Opponent is reacting defensively (not simply driving forward).
- The opponent is actively trying to:
  - bring legs together (“knee pinch / leg closure”), and
  - occupy/pack the space you need at the ankle line (“fill ankle space”).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- You prevent or re-open the leg-closure (knees cannot stay glued).
- You clear ankle-space packing so your pummel lane returns.
- You can resume:
  - base-break invariant (hands-to-mat), and then
  - pummel through to exposure, or
  - switch/spin-under to a different lane.
- You deny the opponent’s escape sequence (pull-away/backstep) while you clear the defense.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**DETECT LEG-CLOSURE + ANKLE-PACK → OPEN THE KNEES → CLEAR ANKLE SPACE → RE-ALIGN KNEE ACROSS HIP → RE-LOCK → ROUTE**

Two problems, two fixes:
- **Leg closure fix:** prevent knees closing via post/structure; keep separation.
- **Ankle-space fix:** clear the packed space so the pummel hook can pass through.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Leg-Closure Gate (pummel denial)
If knees are closed, pummel is functionally off.
- **Default action:** post with second hand to keep knees apart, then re-establish knee-reap alignment.

### 5.2 Ankle-Space Packing Gate (lane denial)
If they fill the ankle space, you cannot thread the hook/front foot correctly.
- **Default action:** adjust angle and re-seat the wedge so the ankle space re-opens; do not insist on pummel through a packed lane.

### 5.3 Backstep Detection Gate (time-critical)
Leg-closure defense often pairs with an escape attempt (backstep/pull-away).
- **Default action:** center-line foot to govern distance first; then clear leg closure/ankle space.

### 5.4 Anchor Preservation Gate (no empty clearing)
You cannot clear these defenses by “letting go and chasing.”
- **Default action:** maintain calf clamp (wrist depth) OR maintain wedge/center-line foot as the anchor while clearing.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Knees pinch together as you try to pummel**  
   → **Default:** second-hand post to keep knees apart; re-seat knee across hip.

2) **You feel “no lane” at the ankle line (space is packed)**  
   → **Default:** re-angle and re-seat wedge; clear ankle space before pummel.

3) **Opponent pinches + begins to backstep**  
   → **Default:** center-line foot distance governance first; then clear pinch/pack.

4) **Opponent pinches + tries pull-away**  
   → **Default:** lock posture (knee-to-floor/clamp) to deny distance; then clear.

5) **You clear the pinch but lane still feels blocked**  
   → **Default:** ankle-space is still packed; re-seat wedge and re-angle.

6) **Repeated pinch/pack keeps happening**  
   → **Default:** run target switch/spin-under module after clearing once; do not repeatedly ram the same lane.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Leg closure (knees pinch):** post with second hand; keep separation; re-align knee across hip.
- **Ankle space filled:** re-angle and re-seat wedge; clear the packed space before pummel.
- **Backstep while pinching:** center-line foot; follow foot; then clear.
- **Pull-away while pinching:** lock posture; deny distance; then clear.
- **Grip strip attempt during clearing:** re-clamp wrist-deep; no finger fighting.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“I keep trying to pummel and it never threads.”** → you’re pummeling through a packed lane; clear ankle space first.
- **“He pinches knees and kills everything.”** → you skipped the second-hand post; prevent closure first.
- **“He backsteps and escapes during my clearing.”** → you didn’t govern distance first; center-line foot earlier.
- **“I let go to clear and he disengaged.”** → anchor preservation failure; keep at least one hard anchor.

## 9. Trigger Recognition Checklist
You are in this module when 2+ cues are true:
- [ ] knees are pinching together (leg closure)
- [ ] your pummel lane feels blocked at ankle line (space packed)
- [ ] your hook keeps getting “stuffed” before it threads
- [ ] opponent starts backstep/pull-away while pinching

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
- [ ] you kept at least one anchor (calf clamp or wedge/center-line foot)
- [ ] knees are separated (pinch is prevented)
- [ ] ankle space is re-opened (lane feels available)
- [ ] knee is re-aligned across your hip before pummel attempts
- [ ] escape is denied (distance governed) while you clear

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Pinch-Prevention Landmark
- **Start:** RDLR contact; opponent’s only defense is to pinch knees closed.
- **Defender wins:** prevent closure (second-hand post) and hold separation + clamp for 5s.
- **Passer wins:** achieve full knee pinch and stall you for 5s.
- **Rules:** no pummel attempts; only separation control.
- **Reset:** 5s hold either side.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Ankle-Space Clearance (no-ramming rule)
- **Start:** opponent packs ankle space; you begin with clamp and wedge.
- **Defender wins:** re-angle/re-seat wedge until lane opens; then perform one clean pummel thread (pause 2s) within 10s.
- **Passer wins:** keep lane packed for 10s.
- **Rules:** any “ram pummel into packed space” attempt is rep loss.
- **Reset:** 2s pause or timer.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Pinch + Backstep Combo (distance first)
- **Start:** opponent pinches and immediately backsteps on cue.
- **Defender wins:** center-line foot first, then open knees/clear ankle space and re-lock within 10s.
- **Passer wins:** complete escape within 10s.
- **Rules:** any clearing attempt before center-line foot placement = rep loss.
- **Reset:** re-lock or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
