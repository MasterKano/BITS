# Governance — Anti-Backstep (Deny Rotation-Clear Exits)

## 1. Purpose
This module governs the common universal escape: **backstep / rotation-clear**.
The opponent uses hip rotation to:
- clear leg ties/entanglements,
- bypass frames,
- or convert into a dominant angle (often backside/side-control style lanes).

This page defines:
- recognition cues,
- denial priorities,
- and the correct routing when denial fails.

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You are engaged in any exchange where rotation can clear the position:
  - open guard (HQ/RDLR/DLR style lanes),
  - leg entanglements (hip turn to clear knee line),
  - passing exchanges (backstep to beat frames/legs).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
- Opponent does not complete a clean backstep/rotation-clear.
- You preserve **facing + contact** while denying the hip turn.
- If they rotate anyway, you route immediately into a controlled containment or reset (no scrambling).

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**READ ROTATION CUE → BLOCK HIP TURN → KEEP FACING → RE-SEAT CONTACT → ROUTE (contain / stabilize / reset)**

Principle: Backstep wins when you are **late** and the opponent’s hips clear your line.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Facing Gate (primary)
If you lose facing while they rotate, you lose the exchange.
- **Default action:** re-square hips/shoulders first; follow rotation while facing.

### 5.2 Hip-Clear Gate (hard failure line)
If their hips clear your controlling line (you’re no longer connected to hip/leg line), denial is too late.
- **Default action:** stop fighting the old position; route to containment/reset immediately.

### 5.3 Contact Integrity Gate
If contact breaks during rotation, you’re chasing.
- **Default action:** keep one meaningful anchor (leg line, hip line, or wrist line depending on domain).

### 5.4 Don’t-Freeze Gate
Pausing to “hold grips” while hips rotate is a loss.
- **Default action:** move your angle with the rotation; re-seat the structure.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Early cue: opponent loads hips and turns knee line away**  
   → **Default:** block hip turn; follow with facing.

2) **Opponent backsteps to clear your legs/frames**  
   → **Default:** keep facing; track hips; re-seat connection rather than “hold and hope.”

3) **Opponent rotates to create backside lane**  
   → **Default:** deny hip clear; if they get it, route to backside stability/containment.

4) **Opponent rotates out of a leg entanglement**  
   → **Default:** prevent knee-line clear via wedge/angle; if clearing, route to containment hub rather than chase.

5) **Rotation creates distance (almost disengage)**  
   → **Default:** clamp one anchor; deny two-step escape (anti-flee interface).

6) **Backstep completes (late)**  
   → **Default:** accept transition; route into your next best defensive structure (no scramble).

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Hip turn begins:** follow with facing; block clearance; re-seat.
- **Backstep mid-pass:** keep frames/legs active; angle with them; prevent hip clear.
- **Knee-line turn-out (leglocks):** prioritize wedge/knee-line integrity; upgrade to containment if needed.
- **Disengage after backstep:** clamp anchor; deny two steps; reset/re-capture if broken.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“He backstepped and I froze.”** → don’t-freeze gate violated; move with rotation.
- **“He cleared hips and I kept fighting the old position.”** → hip-clear gate violated; route immediately.
- **“I got turned away.”** → facing gate violated; re-square first, then follow.
- **“He rotated and disengaged.”** → contact integrity gate violated; keep a meaningful anchor.

## 9. Key Cues (recognition)
- hip load + leg retract + outward hip turn,
- step-behind action (backstep) to clear your legs,
- opponent’s knee line turning away from your control,
- sudden angle change that threatens backside.

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
- [ ] I recognized rotation early (before hips cleared)
- [ ] I stayed facing while following
- [ ] I blocked hip clearance (not just hands)
- [ ] I kept one meaningful anchor during transition
- [ ] I routed quickly if denial failed

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Rotation Denial Reps
- **Start:** engaged position (partner has permission to backstep/rotate).
- **Defender wins:** deny hip clear for 5s and maintain facing throughout.
- **Passer wins:** complete rotation-clear to a clean exit lane within 5s.
- **Rules:** defender must move with rotation; no freezing.
- **Reset:** timer or clear.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Backstep → Disengage Chain
- **Start:** partner backsteps then immediately tries to disengage to two steps.
- **Defender wins:** deny disengage (keep anchor) for 10s.
- **Passer wins:** achieve two clean steps with no meaningful contact.
- **Rules:** defender must keep facing during the rotation phase.
- **Reset:** two-step success or 10s denial.
- **Rounds:** 6 reps each, switch.
