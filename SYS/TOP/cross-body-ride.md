# Cross-body ride

## Purpose

A top control hub used to:
- stabilize off backsteps/rolls (including leg entanglement scramble resolutions),
- deny re-guarding by controlling hip line + shoulder line,
- create reliable pass/back branching without giving up position in transitions.

## Core idea

- Cross-body ride is a **connection-first** platform.
- It is the preferred stabilizer when:
  - the opponent is rotating/rolling and you must stay connected,
  - a forward step pin (north-south) is not yet available,
  - you need a “ride” that lets you follow movement without losing hips.

## Key positions

- Cross-body ride (primary hub)
  - You are “riding” their rotation with hip/shoulder control rather than pinning flat immediately.
- Ride → camping
  - When rotation slows and you can freeze hips to force the fork.
- Ride → north-south
  - When forward step is available and cranial shift is established.
- Ride → tight waist
  - When turtle/quadpod forms or you need a tighter connector to prevent standing.

## Entries

- Backstep entries (common)
  - When you backstep out of leg entanglements or pass attempts and need a stable landing.
- Roll-follow entries
  - When they roll/invert and you must stay connected rather than “fight the roll.”
- Denied north-south / denied camping
  - If they keep rotating and you cannot freeze hips cleanly yet, ride them first.

## Key cues (operational)

- “Follow the torso, not the feet”: ride the shoulder/hip line through rotation.
- “Do not jump ahead”: if you try to skip to the endpoint, you lose connection and they re-guard.
- “Cranial shift is the upgrade”: ride becomes a pin once head/shoulders are controlled.
- “Knees are the alarm”: if knees begin inserting, your hip line control is failing.

## Control gates (must be true before you branch)

### 1) Shoulder line captured
- They cannot freely rotate shoulders to square without breaking your control first.
- You are not hanging off with only arm contact.

### 2) Hip line controlled
- Their hips cannot slide away to insert knees.
- You can follow hip motion without losing proximity.

### 3) Knee insertion denied
- Knees cannot freely enter between you and torso.
- If knee insertion begins, you re-angle and re-capture hips before advancing.

### 4) Cranial shift present (or being built)
- If head/shoulders are free, expect re-square.
- Upgrade head/shoulder control before attempting to “finish” the pass.

## Decision rules (IF/THEN) — lane-by-lane

### A) Establishment (getting the ride stable)
- IF they are rotating/rolling -> THEN ride first (stay connected), do not try to pin immediately.
- IF you feel your connection loosening -> THEN stop advancing and re-capture shoulder/hip line.
- IF knees are inserting -> THEN re-angle to deny knees before attempting a pin upgrade.

### B) Upgrade selection (which stabilizer next)
- IF rotation slows and hips can be frozen -> THEN transition to camping to force the fork.
- IF forward step is available and cranial shift is present -> THEN transition to north-south to deepen the pin.
- IF turtle/quadpod forms -> THEN tight waist trigger.
- IF you are already stable but cannot force a turn -> THEN increase cranial pressure (build cranial shift) before changing hubs.

### C) Branch: turn-toward (faces/frames)
- IF they turn toward -> THEN commit to chest-to-chest and progress passing.
- IF frames stop your advancement -> THEN re-win cranial control and re-angle; do not drive square through frames.

### D) Branch: turn-away (runs/turns out)
- IF they turn away -> THEN close distance to upper back and attach (connection before hooks).
- IF distance opens -> THEN re-ride and re-close; do not reach for hooks from far.

### E) Branch: turtle
- IF turtle forms -> THEN tight waist is the stabilizer; breakdown first, then branch pass/back.

## Opponent counters + immediate answers

- Counter: continuous rotation to prevent pins.
  - Answer: ride them; do not insist on flat pin until cranial shift is built.
- Counter: knee insertion during your transition.
  - Answer: re-angle and deny knees; your hip line control failed.
- Counter: frames + re-square while you ride.
  - Answer: build cranial shift before upgrading to pass.
- Counter: stand/quadpod during ride.
  - Answer: tight waist trigger; collapse height.

## Failure signatures + fixes

- “I try to pin and they roll out.”
  - Fix: you skipped ride; follow rotation first, then upgrade to pin.
- “They keep getting knees in.”
  - Fix: hip line control failed; deny knee insertion before advancing.
- “I chase hooks and lose them.”
  - Fix: attach upper back first; hooks second; if distance opens, re-ride.
- “Ride turns into a scramble.”
  - Fix: you advanced while gates weren’t satisfied; re-capture shoulder/hip line and rebuild.

## Completion checks

- You can maintain connection through their rotation without giving them space to re-guard.
- You can force a predictable reaction (toward/away/turtle) or upgrade to a deeper stabilizer (camping/north-south/tight waist).
