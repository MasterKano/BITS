```
- Resistance Ladder = suggested progression for class

|---|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------|

```
Resistance Ladder (recommended defaults)
- Static: cooperative reps; partner gives correct shape only
- Prog: 30–60% resistance focused on the specific reaction in the row
- Pos: positional sparring from established back crucifix (goal: hold or finish)
- Reset: if escape occurs, restart immediately (high reps, low downtime)

# Back Crucifix — 6-Week Teaching Plan (2 classes/week)

## Week 1 — System identity + Primary control dial (Far Arm)
**Class 1:** System goal/identity; what “counts” as control (I1–I3). `[V1,V2,V3]`
- Drill rows: 1 (establish), 2 (far arm slip).
- Positional: start in near-established crucifix; top wins by holding 30s.

**Class 2:** Far Arm module deepening + Wedge/Hook/Prop mechanic. `[V2]`
- Drill rows: 2 (far arm slip), 10 (abort finish → rebuild).
- Positional: bottom’s only goal = free far arm.

## Week 2 — Secondary control dial (Near Shoulder) + Organization model (3×6)
**Class 3:** Near shoulder control as a “leak plug.” `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 3 (near shoulder mobility), 4 (alignment degrade).
- Positional: bottom’s goal = turn-in; top must preserve I1.

**Class 4:** “Three by Six” as the stabilization/upgrade map. `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 1 (establish), 4 (alignment degrade), plus short “upgrade then hold” rounds.
- Positional: start slightly unstable; top must stabilize within 15s.

## Week 3 — Problem modules (retention under predictable reactions)
**Class 5:** Seated Arm Sit early warning (prevent) + recover logic. `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 5 (early arm-sit), 6 (late arm-sit).
- Positional: bottom hunts arm-sit; top wins by preventing for 30s.

**Class 6:** Locked hands defense: keep position while opening lock. `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 7 (locked hands), 10 (control degrades during finishing).
- Positional: bottom locks hands immediately; top must open then threaten finish.

## Week 4 — Standing phase + Interfaces
**Class 7:** Opponent rises/stands: preserve invariants under new leverage. `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 8 (standing), 4 (alignment degrade).
- Positional: start with bottom standing; top wins by stabilize + threaten finish.

**Class 8:** Interfaces: Rear mount ↔ Crucifix, Crucifix ↔ T-Kimura (decision-making). `[V3]`
- Drill rows: 11, 12.
- Positional: free transitions; score “good decisions” + “dominant control maintained.”

## Week 5 — Finishing families + Totality Principle
**Class 9:** Finish families overview: Strangles / Garrote / Arm locks. `[V4]`
- Drill rows: 9 (finish stalls → switch), 10 (abort → rebuild).
- Positional: top can only win by submission; bottom wins by escape.

**Class 10:** Totality Principle (chaining rules + return-to-control). `[V4]`
- Drill rows: 9 and “chain-only rounds” (top must attempt at least 2 families).
- Positional: “submission or dominant control” scoring.

## Week 6 — Entries integration + Add-on option
**Class 11:** Entry integration (choose 1–2 entry families appropriate to your students). `[V5–V7]`
- Drill rows: 1 (establish), 2 (far arm slip) starting immediately after entry.
- Positional: start from your chosen entry starting position; goal = reach stable crucifix.

**Class 12:** Add-on: Reverse Omoplata as an option inside finish selection. `[V7]`
- Drill rows: 13 (reverse omoplata), 9 (finish stalls → switch).
- Positional: top must attempt reverse omoplata at least once, but cannot sacrifice control.
