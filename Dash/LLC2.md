## Seq 1: Seated Guard to Cross Ashi

Roll into cross ashi from butterfly guard

1. Catch collar tie, hand post, heist to a knee (off their centre line)
2. Shoot arm across the back to the lat, scoop partner's leg and roll pulling partner's lat
3. Land and heist to top position
4. Catch top leg and sit securing double-trouble

**Notes**

- Dilemma at 1 - if partner does not push back we take top position
- Option at 4 to punch underhook from top position to pass guard and secure top position

## Seq 2: Leg Lace to Texas Cloverleaf to Lat Knee Bar

1. Leg Lace is just pain (not everyone taps to this) and easy counter
2. Texas Cloverleaf is a more solid finish - should be a knee bar but more difficult
3. Leg lace is easy to get into so we 

1. From leg lace scoop grip we pull foot across our body and we drop down to shoulder
2. pass to v-grip and pass foot behind back for the break
