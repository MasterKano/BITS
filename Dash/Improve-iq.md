### 69kg | 45-Year-Old Black Belt | Lightest in Room | Kimura + Legs + Outside Passing

This is not about adding technique.  
This is about compressing decision errors and tightening architecture.

Your goal now:
- Fewer bad decisions
- Fewer energy leaks
- Faster prioritization
- Higher trap density
- Safer long-term progression

---

# SECTION 1 — DECISION HIERARCHY MATRIX

You must know the correct order in your primary systems.

If you skip step 1 and jump to step 3, you leak energy.

---

## A. Outside Passing Hierarchy

1. Head line control  
2. Inside shoulder line  
3. Hip exposure  
4. Leg clearance  
5. Chest stabilization  

**Abort Condition:**  
If you lose head control, reset.

---

## B. Kimura System Hierarchy

1. Elbow isolation  
2. Shoulder rotation control  
3. Hip connection  
4. Reaction trap (roll / posture / frame)  
5. Finish OR transition  

**Abort Condition:**  
If elbow escapes rib line, transition immediately.

---

## C. Leg Entanglement Hierarchy

1. Knee line  
2. Inside leg position  
3. Heel alignment  
4. Breaking mechanics  

**Abort Condition:**  
If knee line is lost, disengage or re-pummel.

---

## D. Back Attack Hierarchy

1. Chest-to-back alignment  
2. Hip line control  
3. Wedge creation  
4. Arm isolation  

**Abort Condition:**  
If chest disconnects, prioritize re-alignment before attacking.

---

### Training Rule:
You may not advance unless the previous variable is secured.

---

# SECTION 2 — FAILURE PATTERN AUDIT

Every 2 weeks, answer:

- Where do heavier partners reverse me?
- Where does my breathing spike?
- Where do I concede underhooks?
- Where do I stall?
- Where do I overcommit?

Find patterns, not isolated mistakes.

IQ improves by eliminating repeated leaks.

---

# SECTION 3 — TRANSITION-ONLY TRAINING PROTOCOL

You win as a lighter black belt in transitions.

### Drill Format:
- Reset whenever position stabilizes.
- No points for static control.
- Score only transitions.

Focus:
- Entry timing
- Reaction chaining
- Micro-angle adjustments

Goal:
Sharpen your best advantage — fluidity.

---

# SECTION 4 — ENERGY LEAK DIAGNOSTIC

Lighter grapplers lose when they match force.

### Weekly Diagnostic Rounds:
- 70% grip pressure maximum.
- No squeezing for first 2 minutes.
- No pushing exchanges allowed.

If you feel yourself “pushing,” you’re compensating for poor angle.

Replace force with angle or timing.

---

# SECTION 5 — TRAP DENSITY SCORECARD

After rounds, score yourself:

- Did each attack produce 2 predictable reactions?
- Did I create a loop or hit a dead end?
- Did I force movement or chase movement?

Your system should loop:

Outside Pass  
→ Frame  
→ Kimura  
→ Roll  
→ Back  
→ Defense  
→ Leg Entanglement  
→ Disengage  
→ Outside Pass reset  

Remove attacks that don’t feed this loop.

---

# SECTION 6 — PRE-LOADING REACTION TRAINING

Before attacking, silently name expected reaction.

Example:
- “He will posture.”
- “He will roll.”
- “He will stand.”

After round:
Track accuracy.

Prediction accuracy = rising IQ.

If you’re wrong often, your read is weak.

---

# SECTION 7 — TEMPO CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Define 4 phases:

1. Slow Phase (information gathering)  
2. Float Phase (off-balancing / light contact)  
3. Acceleration Phase (commit to transition)  
4. Finish Phase (tight control + submission or stabilization)

Heavier grapplers want chaos.
You want tempo modulation.

Never stay in Acceleration Phase longer than needed.

---

# SECTION 8 — LONGEVITY FILTER

Intelligent grappling includes risk management.

Rules:
- Bail early from deep heel exposure.
- Avoid ego-based 50/50 wars.
- Don’t defend late-stage breaks explosively.

Strategic tap philosophy:
Tap early to joint locks. Preserve training longevity.

IQ includes self-preservation.

---

# SECTION 9 — SYSTEM COMPRESSION

Define:

Primary Entry: Outside pass pressure  
Primary Trap: Kimura isolation  
Primary Reaction Feed: Back exposure  
Primary Reset: Leg entanglement  
Primary Return: Outside passing  

Remove techniques that don’t connect.

Black belt growth now is subtraction.

---

# SECTION 10 — OBJECTIVE IQ TRACKING

Track weekly:

- Number of clean transitions
- Hierarchy violations
- Energy spikes
- Prediction accuracy
- Dead-end attacks

Quantify thinking quality.

# SECTION 11 — 6-WEEK REFINEMENT PROTOCOL

### Weeks 1–2:
- Focus on hierarchy discipline.
- Film 2 rounds per week.
- Identify 1 repeated leak.

### Weeks 3–4:
- Transition-only rounds.
- Trap density audit.
- Remove one dead-end attack.

### Weeks 5–6:
- Pre-load reactions deliberately.
- Tempo phase control.
- Energy diagnostic rounds.

Goal:
Fewer forced scrambles.
More controlled transitions.
