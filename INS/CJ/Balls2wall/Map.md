

# BALLS TO WALL - TECHNIQUE MAP

## Craig Jones Wall Wrestling System

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                            CORE CONCEPTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE: Wall changes everything                 │
    │  • Can't sprawl (legs hit wall)                                 │
    │  • Bent-at-hips shots work better                               │
    │  • ULTIMATE GOAL: Locked Double (hands locked below butt)       │
    │  • Wall prevents back takes BUT helps defense                   │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                         VOLUME 1: FUNDAMENTALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          DEFENSIVE POSITIONS                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    SPLIT SQUAT (Primary Defense)
    ═══════════════════════════════
         Wall
          │
          │  You (knees parallel to wall)
          │    /\
          │   /  \  ← Legs wide, knees facing same direction
          │  /    \
          │ •──────• ← Creates distance around knees
          │
          └─ Prevents locked double


    REVERSE SPLIT SQUAT
    ═══════════════════
         Wall
          │
          │  You (opposite stance)
          │   \  /
          │    \/  ← Easier to run out of
          │    │
          │


    DEFENSIVE SHRUGS & GRIPS
    ════════════════════════
    1. Overhook/Whizzer (high and tight)
    2. Get nice and low
    3. Shrug opponent up above hips
    4. Control inside space:
       • Start at bicep → slide to wrist
       • Keep elbow tight to hip
       • Prevent body lock attempts


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         OFFENSIVE POSITIONS                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    KEEPING THEM AGAINST WALL
    ═════════════════════════
              Wall
               │
               │ ← Their back
            [HEAD]  ← Win head position (outside)
               │
          [KNEE]    ← Knee inside their thigh
               │
              You


    KEY POINTS:
    • Head to outside (close the door)
    • Knee inside their thigh
    • Drive pressure constantly
    • Control inside hand position


    MAX HOLLOWAY HIGH WHIZZER FRAME
    ════════════════════════════════
    • High overhook/whizzer
    • Frame against their head
    • Keep head pressed away
    • Makes it hard to pin you to cage


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                          VOLUME 2: ATTACKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        SINGLE LEG SERIES                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    SINGLE LEG → BODY LOCK
    ══════════════════════
    1. Shoot double, realize it's not there
    2. Switch to single leg (keeps you pinning them)
    3. Step behind their knee with your knee
    4. Run the pipe (shoulder on top technique)
    5. When they lose balance → hands open
    6. Lock body lock → run back to cage


    SINGLE LEG → LOCKED DOUBLE
    ══════════════════════════
    1. Get single leg position
    2. Step behind their knee
    3. Pull them away from wall
    4. As they press back flat against wall:
       • Their knees point forward
       • Lock your hands around legs
       • Hit locked double finish


    HEAD OUTSIDE SINGLE (MMA Specific)
    ══════════════════════════════════
    • Shoot from distance, head outside
    • Works because you can drive to cage
    • Elbow-deep grip (RNC style)
    • Pull away from cage
    • Threaten dump → lock double


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         BODY LOCK ATTACKS                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    BODY LOCK BASICS
    ════════════════
    Grip: C-grip (palm up on outside hand)
    • Outside elbow stays tight
    • Hard for them to re-whizzer
    • Step inside and rotate to take back


    IF THEY KEEP WHIZZER
    ═══════════════════
    You step inside → They go flat (full takedown)


    IF THEY RELEASE WHIZZER (Smart)
    ════════════════════════════════
    They post hands → Expose back → Back take position


    KAMARU USMAN KNEE BLOCK
    ═══════════════════════
    1. Body lock secured
    2. Step inside (they might block with their leg)
    3. Take knee PAST their block
    4. Shelf it on your hip
    5. Lift and turn


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          FOOT SWEEPS                                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    THREE MAIN FOOT SWEEPS:
    ═══════════════════════

    1. INSIDE SWEEP
       ────────────
              Wall
               │
            [Foot] ← Target foot
               │
       [Your Foot] ← Kick in line with toes, then out
               

    2. OUTSIDE HOOK (Kosoto)
       ─────────────────────
              Wall
               │
            [Foot] ← Hook behind with your calf
               │
           [Hook] ← Drag backwards/upwards
               

    3. INSIDE TRIP
       ───────────
              Wall
               │
            [Foot] ← Step inside
               │
           [Trip] ← Pull away from wall


    FOOT SWEEP → LOCK DOUBLE CHAIN
    ═══════════════════════════════
    • Sweep foot out
    • They push back against wall to stay up
    • Both knees now point forward
    • Lock the double!


    FOOT SWEEP → BACK TAKE (from Body Lock)
    ════════════════════════════════════════
    • Outside hook/Kosoto
    • They feel takedown coming
    • Release whizzer and turn to cage
    • You take the back


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    VOLUME 3: DEFENSE & GETTING UP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    DEFENSIVE BASE (When Taken Down)                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    THE BOUNCE BACK CONCEPT
    ═══════════════════════
    MOST IMPORTANT: Hit ground → Bounce immediately to wall
    • Don't pause for even half a second
    • Win the first battle (saves energy later)
    • Protect the space behind your hips


    BASE POSITION PROGRESSION:
    ══════════════════════════

    Stage 1: BUTT DOWN
    ──────────────────
              Wall
               ║
               ║ ← Back against wall
          [SEATED]
            /    \  ← Feet tucked, protecting hip space
           •      •


    Stage 2: HAND UP
    ────────────────
              Wall
               ║
           [HAND] ← Post hand on wall
               ║
          [KNEE] ← One knee up
               ║


    Stage 3: STANDING
    ─────────────────
              Wall
               ║
          [STANDING] ← Feet back toward wall
               ║      Head against wall if needed
               ║      Drive into wall


    CRITICAL: PROTECT THE SPACE
    ════════════════════════════
    • Don't let them get knee inside
    • Don't let them get hook in
    • Drive HARD into wall (never relaxed)
    • Use wall as platform


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        GRIP DEFENSE (Standing)                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    DIFFERENT GRIPS = DIFFERENT RESPONSES
    ═════════════════════════════════════

    GABLE GRIP (Easier to break, easier to lift)
    ────────────────────────────────────────────
    Response: Hip pop → Break grip → Arm drag


    C-GRIP / S-GRIP (Harder to break, harder to lift)
    ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Response: Slip elbow inside → Be patient → Wait for opening


    GENERAL PRINCIPLE:
    • Keep overhook high (prevents re-shoots)
    • Control inside space (prevents body lock)
    • Stay disciplined
    • Let them take risks out of frustration


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     OFFENSIVE FOLLOW-UPS (Top)                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    HEAD HEIGHT BATTLE
    ══════════════════
              Wall
               ║
          [YOUR HEAD] ← Must be ABOVE their head
               ║
          [Their Head] ← Stapled, can't turn
               ║

    • Most important offensive concept
    • Prevents them building height
    • Prevents turtle in either direction
    • Enables wrist control and dragging


    LEG SHELVING
    ════════════
    • Elevate their leg off ground
    • Makes it tough to stand
    • Place your weight over it
    • Can be: closed guard, half guard, or foot trap


    DRAGGING AWAY FROM WALL
    ═══════════════════════
    • Reverse grip on ankle (stronger)
    • Pull them away from cage
    • They scoot back → You get dominant position
    • Chase with knee inside


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                      VOLUME 4: ADVANCED TECHNIQUES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      REAR BODY LOCK CHALLENGES                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    PROBLEM: Hard to lift when they press back into wall
    ════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    
    FOOT SWEEP SOLUTION
    ───────────────────
    • They drive back into wall
    • Attack far leg with foot sweep
    • Especially when they step up from knee


    MISDIRECTION LIFT (Head to Wall)
    ────────────────────────────────
    1. Fake commitment to one side
    2. They put weight on that leg
    3. Step through on opposite side
    4. Lift


    KOSOTO HOOK (Knee Inside)
    ─────────────────────────
    • Get knee pinched inside
    • Outside hook their far leg
    • Drag them to hip
    • Great back exposure


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    SEATBELT VS REAR BODY LOCK                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    THE SEATBELT SURPRISE
    ═════════════════════
    Problem: Everyone trains to defend rear body lock
    Solution: Use seatbelt (less common, different defense)

    SEATBELT SAG TECHNIQUE:
    ──────────────────────
    1. Seatbelt grip (one over, one under)
    2. Squat and SAG weight
    3. Lift their far shoulder
    4. They try to knee slide → Go to hip instead
    5. Burns their abs if they resist


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         ARM DRAG DEFENSE                                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    LEON EDWARDS TECHNIQUE
    ══════════════════════
    Timing: Right as they shoot (early)

    1. As they shoot, slip arm INSIDE
    2. Hit split squat
    3. Grip: Pull their hand below their armpit
       • If hand is in FRONT of armpit: They can retract
       • If hand is BELOW armpit: Can't retract
    4. Prevent all hand locking attempts


    TWO EXITS:
    ─────────
    A. REVERSE SPLIT SQUAT
       • Cross-step and turn
       • Run out of position

    B. PROPER ARM DRAG
       • Turn feet toward them
       • Drag past
       • Re-shoot as they turn back


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      HIGH-RISK TECHNIQUES                               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    NINJA CHOKE
    ═══════════
    Setup: Arm drag position
    1. They drive low and tight
    2. Snake arm through on drag side
    3. Cover their head (prevent posture)
    4. Rear-naked style grip
    5. Throttle choking arm


    ROLLING TO LEGS
    ═══════════════
    Risky but effective against cage:
    1. They have rear body lock
    2. Their back to cage (you know where legs are)
    3. Grab both ankles
    4. Roll through
    5. Attack legs (knee bar, heel hook)


    OVERSTRETCH FOOT SWEEPS (Wizard Counter)
    ════════════════════════════════════════
    1. Keep wizard pressure
    2. Bring front leg forward (not full split)
    3. They overstretch knee inside
    4. Grab far armpit + lift knee = sweep
    OR: Commit fully and create knee shelf


    FLYING TRIANGLE
    ═══════════════
    Setup: Low posture double leg
    1. Post hand on ground
    2. Lift knee HIGH
    3. Put all weight on their posting arm
    4. Slide leg through for triangle
    5. (Can also hit armbar)


    ISLAM'S GLOVE GRAB
    ══════════════════
    Sneaky technique from Combat Sambo:
    1. Start with wrist/elbow control
    2. Battle for grips
    3. Snake fingers INSIDE glove
    4. Hidden from ref
    5. Use for throws (Uchimata, Harai Goshi)
    6. Can also use in over-under positions

    Counter (Volkanovski): Take underhook inside immediately


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                          TRAINING METHODS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    POSITIONAL SPARRING POSITIONS:
    ══════════════════════════════

    Beginner Defense: Start with legs not shelved
    Advanced Defense: Start with legs shelved, low position
    Worst Case: Start with them having wrist control

    Offense: Start from various grips (single, double, underhook)


    BOUNCE BACK TRAINING:
    ═════════════════════
    • Partner completes perfect takedown
    • The SECOND you hit ground → GO
    • Emphasizes winning first battle
    • Most important wall wrestling concept


    KEY CONCEPTS TO DRILL:
    ══════════════════════
    ✓ Never pause when hit ground
    ✓ Protect space behind hips at all costs
    ✓ Head height battles (offense and defense)
    ✓ Chaining attacks (foot sweep → lock double)
    ✓ Staying disciplined with grips
    ✓ Using wall as friend (defense) and platform (escape)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

                    CRAIG'S GOLDEN RULES:

    1. Wall changes EVERYTHING - rethink all wrestling
    2. Locked double is the ultimate goal (offense)
    3. Split squat is the ultimate defense
    4. Win the bounce back battle (most important)
    5. Head height = control
    6. Protect the space behind your hips like your life depends on it
    7. Things are simpler against the wall (fewer options, must be better)
    8. Foot sweeps set up everything else
    9. Never pause when you hit the ground
    10. Guard against the wall = terrible position
```
