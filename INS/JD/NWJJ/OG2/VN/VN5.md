# Hub — Inside Senkaku: Forward Barrier (Stabilize Knee Line, Deny Turn-Away)

## 1. Purpose
Inside Senkaku is a leg-entanglement stabilization hub. Its job is to convert a leg entry into a **forward barrier** that:
- locks the **knee line** (prevents knee retraction),
- denies the opponent’s **turn-away/run** escape,
- and forces predictable reactions that enable either (a) controlled finishing work or (b) routing to a safer containment hub.

Operational intent: **stabilize first, finish second.**

## 2. Inputs / Preconditions
- You have arrived in an inside entanglement where a forward barrier is structurally possible:
  - your legs can block the opponent’s hip/thigh line forward,
  - and you can engage the knee line (or immediately win it).
- Opponent is attempting one of:
  - **turn-away/run** (rotate away and flee),
  - **backstep/rotate out** (hip turns to clear),
  - **heavy-leg stuffing** (weights your structure),
  - **pull-away** (disengage to distance).

## 3. Outputs / Success Criteria
You are “in hub” when all are true:
- the opponent cannot retract the knee past your barrier (no knee-line escape),
- turn-away stalls within 1–2 steps,
- you can hold the state **3 seconds** without scrambling,
- you have a clean route to either a finish system or a containment hub.

## 4. Core Model / Engine
**ARRIVE → BUILD FORWARD BARRIER → WIN KNEE LINE → DENY TURN-AWAY → CONSOLIDATE → ROUTE / FINISH**

Forward barrier operational definition:
- your leg structure blocks the opponent’s hip from clearing away,
- while your connection prevents free rotation out.

## 5. Gates (override rules)

### 5.1 Knee Line Gate (primary)
If knee line is not secured, inside senkaku is not real.
- **Default action:** re-seat barrier + re-angle hips + tighten wedge until knee cannot retract.

### 5.2 Turn-Away Gate (time-critical)
If the opponent begins turn-away/run, denial must be immediate.
- **Default action:** keep barrier forward; clamp and follow rotation; do not let hip clear your frame.

### 5.3 Heavy-Leg Gate (stuffing defense)
If the opponent weights/stuffs your structure, your barrier loses function.
- **Default action:** change angle (hip scoot/angle reset) to remove their weight line; rebuild barrier.

### 5.4 Pull-Away Gate (disengage)
If disengage begins, chasing is a loss condition.
- **Default action:** lock containment; if the line breaks, route to 50-50/double-50 rather than chase.

### 5.5 One-Step Clear Gate (integrity test)
If the opponent can clear entanglement in one step/one rotation, you are not consolidated.
- **Default action:** re-seat lower and add redundancy before any finishing attempt.

## 6. Top-6 Situations Playbook
1) **Arrive but knee line feels loose**  
   → **Default:** win knee line first (no attacks).

2) **Immediate turn-away/run**  
   → **Default:** deny turn-away (barrier forward; clamp; follow).

3) **Backstep/rotate out**  
   → **Default:** track hip rotation; keep barrier; re-seat knee line.

4) **Heavy-leg stuffing**  
   → **Default:** re-angle to remove weight line; rebuild barrier.

5) **Pull-away attempt**  
   → **Default:** containment first; route to 50-50/double-50 if needed.

6) **Stable knee line + barrier**  
   → **Default:** route to finish system or to containment hub per selection rules.

## 7. Opponent Reactions → Responses
- **Turn-away/run:** barrier stays forward; clamp; follow rotation; deny hip clearance.
- **Backstep/rotate:** track hip; keep barrier; re-seat; prevent step-clear.
- **Heavy-leg:** re-angle; rebuild; do not grip-fight against weight.
- **Pull-away:** lock containment; if break occurs, route to safer containment hub.
- **Hand-fight/strip:** replace grips without losing knee line; structural control > hand chasing.

## 8. Failure Signatures → Fix
- **“He ran away.”** → turn-away gate violated; deny earlier with barrier-forward + clamp.
- **“He pulled knee out.”** → knee line not secured; win knee line first.
- **“He pinned my legs heavy.”** → heavy-leg gate violated; re-angle to remove weight line.
- **“He disengaged as I chased.”** → pull-away gate violated; lock or route.

## 9. Key Cues

### 9.1 Permission Cues (enter hub mode)
- You can build a forward barrier across the hip/thigh line.
- Knee line is captured or can be captured immediately.
- Turn-away is not already “free” (hip has not fully cleared).

### 9.2 Abort Cues (route away)
- Knee line retracts repeatedly despite re-seating.
- Hip has already cleared and opponent is running (late turn-away).
- Heavy-leg pins your structure and you cannot re-angle.

### 9.3 Success Landmarks
- Knee cannot retract past your barrier.
- Turn-away stalls within 1–2 steps.
- 3-second stable hold is achievable without scrambling.

## 10. Quality / Capture Checklist
- [ ] knee line secured (no retract)
- [ ] forward barrier structurally present (hip cannot clear)
- [ ] turn-away denied on first cue
- [ ] heavy-leg solved by angle change
- [ ] pull-away handled by lock or route (no chase)
- [ ] one-step clear is denied

## 13. Drills and Games

### 13.1 Knee-Line First (no attacking early)
- **Start:** inside senkaku entry with knee line contestable.
- **Defender wins:** secure knee line and hold hub state 3s.
- **Passer wins:** retract knee line and disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** any finishing attempt before 3s knee-line control = rep loss.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 10 reps each, switch.

### 13.2 Turn-Away Denial
- **Start:** hub state; opponent’s only action is immediate turn-away/run.
- **Defender wins:** deny turn-away for 3s (stall within 1–2 steps).
- **Passer wins:** turn-away escape/disengage within 5s.
- **Rules:** barrier stays forward; no hand-only chasing.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or escape.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.

### 13.3 Heavy-Leg Stuffing Solve
- **Start:** hub state; opponent heavy-legs to pin your barrier.
- **Defender wins:** re-angle and rebuild barrier within 5s, then hold 3s.
- **Passer wins:** pin/stall 5s without allowing re-angle.
- **Rules:** must solve by angle change, not grip fighting.
- **Reset:** 3s hold or 5s stall.
- **Rounds:** 8 reps each, switch.
