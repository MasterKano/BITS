    |
    v
[1] PRIMARY GAME = BATTLE FOR POSTURE
    - Aim: break posture by removing posts + knee pull (legs are primary engine) 
    - "Winning signals" include: hands to mat (fwd/back), chest-to-chest, hips to mat, shoulders below hips :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
    |
    +------------------------------+
    |                              |
    v                              v
[1A] YOU WIN POSTURE-BREAK         [1B] YOU LOSE POSTURE-BREAK (they posture/stand away)
     (base broken)                      "Golden rule": to posture, they must leave legs behind 
     |                                     -> legs get long + weight to heels 
     v
[2] STEP TWO = ENTER DOMINANT POSITION (height + angle metric) 
     |
     +------------------------------+
     |                              |
     v                              v
[2A] STRONGLY DOMINANT              [2B] WEAKLY DOMINANT
     (extremes of height/angle)          (usable when you can't get extremes) 
     |
     |-- (Hub) SIDE SCISSOR
     |      - invariant: get/stay outside elbow 
     |      - tactical core = TRILEMMA:
     |          (i) Height win -> come up / back exposure 
     |         (ii) Opponent head over you -> loses post -> sweep to no-post side 
     |        (iii) Opponent recovers elbow around body -> you enter CLAMP -> submissions 
     |
     |-- (Branch off clamp) CLAMP SYSTEM
     |      - side clamp vs vertical clamp as opponent defends height/angle 
     |      - clamp -> triangle/trimura/armbar families (examples shown) 
     |
     |-- DOUBLE UNDERHOOKS (paired with posture break + angle work)
     |      - concept: legs lock hips + arms lock head/shoulders; denies guard opening :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}
     |      - common entry logic: off-balance -> hands to mat -> space under arms -> lock up 
     |
     |-- "SITTING DUCK" / SCOOP SWEEP OUTCOME
     |      - knock standing opponent down -> seated base collapse -> you sit on hips (height advantage) 
     |
     v
[3] STEP THREE = FINISH / SWEEP / PROGRESS
    - If you can cycle among dominant positions, opponent becomes purely defensive 

[2B] WEAKLY DOMINANT SUB-HUBS (when extremes unavailable)
    |
    |-- TOP LOCK (convert low lock to high lock behind head/shoulder -> enables juji/triangles) 
    |
    |-- TRAP TRIANGLE (crossed-feet triangle control high on upper back/neck -> transitions) 
    |
    |-- ELBOW OVERHOOK (kneeling/standing; foot over head + extra fulcrum behind elbow -> tight arm lock) 

[1B] IF YOU LOSE POSTURE (they posture/stand successfully): FAILURE-BRANCH (system-required)
    |
    v
[4] LOWER-BODY COMPENSATION (2-way)
    A) LEG LOCKS: posture win forces legs long -> target legs 
    B) BACKWARD SWEEPS: weight to heels -> knock to butt / sweep rearward 
       - "rule of thumb": control both legs + push -> they fall to butt :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21}
